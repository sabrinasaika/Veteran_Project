B. Use of FRRP Funds
1. Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset
losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based
on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
Alaska’s three Blind Vendors are required to submit a monthly financial 
statement to the SLA that summarizes sales, expenses, and profits. The SLA did a 
comparison of the year-end numbers for both calendar year 2019 and 2020. The 
2020 total profits were subtracted from the 2019 profits to determine if there was 
a loss in the calendar year 2020 and, if so, the exact amount of that loss. From this 
total, the SLA subtracted Fair Minimum Return payments made during 2020 to 
determine losses that were eligible for FRRP reimbursement. Two vendors will 
receive a proportional percentage of the available FRRP numbers based upon the 
percentage of their losses compared to the total losses of all vendors. 
 
Our third vendor did not experience any loss during this period. She had an 
increase in revenue in 2020. 
 
Under no circumstances will a Blind Vendor receive more in FRRP funds than 
their calculated losses.

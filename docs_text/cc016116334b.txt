RE: Federal Fiscal Year 2023 Maintenance of Effort Reduction 
 
Dear Meredith Dressel: 
 
On May 8, 2025, the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) issued a letter to the State of 
Wisconsin regarding it not satisfying the maintenance of effort (MOE) requirement under the 
State Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VR) program during Federal fiscal year (FFY) 2023 
and, as a result, of the need to reduce the Wisconsin Department of Workforce Development 
(DWD) grant for FFY 2026 by $843,122, the amount of the MOE shortfall. RSA calculated the 
MOE shortfall based on information contained in the original Federal Financial Reports (RSA-
17) submitted by the Wisconsin Department of Workforce Development (DWD). 
 
After receiving RSA’s letter, DWD notified RSA that the Quarter 4 and Quarter 8 RSA-17 
reports submitted for FFYs 2021 through 2024 were incorrect. On May 20, 2025, RSA 
conducted a conference call with DWD staff to determine what happened and discuss next steps. 
DWD's internal controls had failed to ensure the correct reporting of the State and Federal share 
of unliquidated obligations. 
 
RSA requested DWD submit a spreadsheet that compared the financial data originally submitted 
on the RSA-17 forms with the revised corrected financial data the agency wanted to submit. On 
May 29, 2025, DWD submitted the requested spreadsheet. RSA reviewed the spreadsheet and 
agreed with the proposed corrections.  
 
RSA opened the financial reports in the RSAMIS so that DWD could revise the original RSA-17 
data. Upon completion, RSA analyzed the revised data to reassess DWD’s standing regarding 
MOE. The financial data demonstrated that DWD no longer had a MOE deficit for FFY 2023. 
Therefore, RSA will not reduce DWD’s FFY 2026 VR award to address a MOE shortfall. See 
the attached table titled “MOE Waiver Calculations” for details.

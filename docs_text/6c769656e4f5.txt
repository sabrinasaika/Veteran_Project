RE: Federal Fiscal Year 2021 Maintenance of Effort Deficit 
Dear Joseph Baxter and Cassondra Williams: 
The Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) issued a letter on July 31, 2024 formally 
notifying your agencies that the State of Arkansas (Arkansas) did not satisfy the maintenance of 
effort (MOE) requirement under the State Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VR) program 
during Federal fiscal year (FFY) 2021 and, as a result, the need to reduce the Division of 
Services for the Blind (DSB) grant for FFY 2025 by the amount of the MOE shortfall (see 
attached letter dated July 31, 2024). However, when RSA issued the FFY 2025 VR awards DSB 
received the full FFY 2025 VR allotment of $5,947,215.00. The grant award to DSB was not 
reduced by $413,527.00 FFY 2021 MOE shortfall. 
This letter is to formerly notify you of the need to reduce DSB’s FFY 2026 VR program grant by 
$413,527.00, the amount of the FFY 2021 MOE shortfall. The Rehabilitation Services 
Administration (RSA) calculated the MOE shortfall based on information contained in the 
Federal Financial Reports (SF-425/RSA-17) submitted by DSB and Arkansas Rehabilitation 
Services (ARS) as described in the attached letter.

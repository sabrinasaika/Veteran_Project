Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
The total amount of appropriated relief funds provided to MCB by RSA will be divided by the total number of eligible Vendors to ascertain the Initial Per Vendor Allocation Amount (IPVAA). Vendors showing total 2020 business losses equal to or less than the IPVAA will be fully compensated to the exact amount of their losses. Remaining relief funds will be evenly divided among Vendors that have reported business losses greater than the IPVAA. No Vendor will receive more relief funds than the amount for which they are eligible. This process of dividing remaining relief funds will continue until all funding has been allocated to Vendors.

How will the SLA ensure blind vendor losses were not previously compensated (e.g., attestation, financial reports, inventory reports, documentation of losses due to spoilage, etc.)?
The following two calculations must be made to arrive at the total projected income per Vendor for calendar year 2020:
1. Determining the net income each Massachusetts Randolph Sheppard (RS) vendor should have realized in calendar year 2020 if the COVID-19 pandemic had not occurred, is the starting point to calculate the amount of Financial Relief and Restoration Payments (FRRP) each Vendor is eligible to receive. MCB will obtain this figure by computing the total of each Vendor’s last 52 weeks of reported business net income prior to January 1, 2020. For Vendors assigned to a vending facility in 2020 that were operating a sister facility for a portion of 2019, Vendor business net earnings will be derived by using reported 2019 net income data summed with an average weekly net income figure to achieve the last 52 weeks of estimated net income prior to January 1, 2020. For Vendors assigned to a vending facility in 2020 that did not operate a facility in 2019, Vendor business net earnings will be derived by using the last 52 weeks of historical net income prior to January 1, 2020 from that location. Where no historical net earnings exist from a location a Vendor was assigned to when the pandemic occurred, estimated annual 2020 business Vendor net earnings shall be obtained by summing the last 52 weeks of reported net income prior to January 1, 2020 from the former facility/facilities the Vendor managed.
2. Vendors that had negotiated 2020 RS related foodservice contracts shall establish a projected net income loss figure based on the negotiated terms of those contracts that will be added to the calculated net income 2020 figure provided by MCB.
The sum of these two figures shall represent the total income that would have been achieved per Vendor in calendar year 2020 if not for the COVID-19 pandemic. Vendors will certify mandatory financial reports submitted to MCB and losses attributed to foodservice contract interruptions are accurate by way of secure online attestation form. MCB will provide this form to each FRRP eligible Vendor and Vendors will affirm via electronic signature.
Adjustment to Projected Income for calendar year 2020
1. Net income for calendar year 2020
2. Fair minimum return payment made to Vendors for pandemic financial relief in calendar year 2020.
Eligible Relief Funds
The eligible relief funds are the difference between the Vendor’s projected income for calendar year 2020 and the adjustment to projected income for calendar year 2020. MCB, with active MECBV participation, defines this eligible relief funds calculation as “compensation for losses”.

RE: FFY 2021 Annual Review Finding Regarding Noncompliance with Pre-Employment 
Transition Services Requirements 
 
Dear Felicia Johnson and Darline Graham: 
 
This letter presents the Rehabilitation Services Administration’s (RSA) annual review and 
finding regarding the State’s failure to satisfy requirements governing the provision of pre-
employment transition services (pre-ETS) in Federal fiscal year (FFY) 2021, as determined 
by RSA in accordance with Section 107(a) of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (Rehabilitation 
Act).
 Pursuant to Section 107(a)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act, RSA conducts an annual review 
of the South Carolina Vocational Rehabilitation Department (SCVRD), and the South 
Carolina Commission for the Blind (SCCB) to determine whether the State is substantially 
complying with the requirements of its Unified or Combined State Plan, including 
requirements governing the provision of pre-ETS to students with disabilities. The FFY 2021 
final financial reports submitted by SCVRD and SCCB, the most recent final financial 
reports available to RSA, demonstrate the State failed to satisfy the requirements of Sections 
110(d)(1) and 113 of the Rehabilitation Act by not expending at least 15 percent of its State 
Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VR) grant amount on the provision of pre-ETS for 
students with disabilities.  
 
Annual Review of SCVRD’s and SCCB’s FFY 2021 Fiscal Data 
 
Short Summary: 
 
RSA has determined, after conducting an annual review of SCVRD’s and SCCB’s final 
FFY 2021 RSA-17 financial reports, pursuant to Section 107(a)(2) of the Rehabilitation Act,

that the agencies failed to satisfy the requirements of Sections 110(d)(1) and 113(a) of the 
Rehabilitation Act (i.e., the expenditure requirements) by not expending at least 15 percent of 
the State’s matched FFY 2021 VR grant funds on the provision of pre-employment transition 
services (pre-ETS) for students with disabilities.  
 
Annual Review Requirements: 
  
Section 107(a)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act requires RSA to conduct annual reviews and 
periodic onsite monitoring of the VR agencies to determine whether they are in substantial 
compliance with their approved State plan, including the VR services portion of that Plan. 
In conducting the annual reviews, RSA must consider, at a minimum, budget, and financial 
management data (Section 107(a)(2)(I) of the Rehabilitation Act). Pursuant to Section 
107(a)(4)(B) of the Rehabilitation Act, RSA must examine the provision of services, 
including pre-ETS.  
 
Analysis of FFY 2021 Financial Data: 
 
According to SCVRD’s and SCCB’s final FFY 2021 RSA-17s, the combined net Federal VR 
allotment for the State was $66,776,414, all of which SCVRD and SCCB matched. 
Therefore, SCVRD and SCCB were required to expend a combined total of at least 
$10,016,462.10 for the provision of pre-ETS. SCVRD and SCCB reported on their respective 
final FFY 2021 RSA-17s spending a combined total of $9,846,033.00 (14.7 percent of the 
State’s VR allotment) in Federal VR funds for pre-ETS. In addition, SCCB had 
$1,203,421.55 in matched unused Federal funds remaining at the end of FFY 2021. 
 
RSA’s Annual Review Finding: 
 
As demonstrated by their final RSA-17s for FFY 2021, SCVRD and SCCB expended a 
combined total of 14.7 percent of their matched Federal VR funds, a decrease from the 15 
percent of the agencies’ expenditures for pre-ETS in FFY 2020. RSA acknowledges SCCB 
continued to reserve the additional .3 percent of its matched Federal VR funds by leaving 
them unspent in SCVRD’s VR account at the end of the period of performance. However, as 
noted above, SCVRD and SCCB spent 14.7 percent on pre-ETS in FFY 2021. 
 
Noncompliance with Approved State Plan: 
 
Section 101(a)(25) of the Rehabilitation Act requires SCVRD and SCCB to assure in their 
Unified or Combined State Plan, with respect to students with disabilities, that the State has 
developed and implemented strategies to: 
 
• address the needs identified in the assessments described in Section 101(a)(15) 
of the Rehabilitation Act; 
• achieve the goals and priorities identified by the State in accordance with Section 
101(a)(15) of the Rehabilitation Act to improve and expand VR services for 
students with disabilities on a statewide basis; and 
• provide pre-ETS.

Pre-ETS, as defined at Section 7(30) of the Rehabilitation Act, “means services provided in 
accordance with section 113.” Section 113(a) of the Rehabilitation Act requires the State to 
ensure that SCVRD and SCCB will use the funds reserved under Section 110(d)(1) of the 
Rehabilitation Act to provide, or arrange for the provision of, pre-ETS to students with 
disabilities. Section 110(d)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act establishes the 15 percent minimum 
reservation requirement for the State from its VR allotment.

Therefore, by signing the VR services portions of their State Plans, both SCVRD and SCCB 
certified that they would administer and operate the State VR program in accordance with the 
State Plan, Rehabilitation Act, applicable regulations, and policies and procedures 
established by the Secretary of Education, which include all requirements governing pre-

described above, the agencies fell short of satisfying these requirements that year. In so 
doing, SCVRD and SCCB also failed to satisfy a routine term and condition that appeared on

Conclusion: 
 
RSA has found, after conducting an annual review of SCVRD’s and SCCB’s final FFY 
2021 RSA-17s pursuant to Section 107 of the Rehabilitation Act, that the State did not 
satisfy the pre-ETS expenditure requirements that year. Specifically, the State reserved 15 
percent of its Federal VR funds and spent 14.7 percent on the provision of pre-ETS for 
students with disabilities. 
For purposes of this finding, RSA requires SCVRD and SCCB continue implementing 
strategies to ensure the State reserves and will expend at least 15 percent of its VR grant 
amount on the provision of pre-employment transition services by September 30, 2024. 
Because the State has reached full compliance with the requirement of Section 110(d)(1) of 
the Rehabilitation Act and, RSA believes, through established strategies, SCVRD and 
SCCB will achieve full compliance with the requirement of Section 113(a) of the 
Rehabilitation Act within a reasonable period of time, RSA requires no further corrective 
actions or submissions. In making this determination, RSA considered that SCVRD and 
SCCB have made significant progress in implementing procedures to bring the State into 
full compliance with the requirement of Section 110(d)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act and 
almost into full compliance with the requirement of Section 113(a) of the Rehabilitation 
Act. 
 
Please be aware that in subsequent audits or program monitoring RSA will review your 
agencies to ensure continued compliance with the applicable requirements.

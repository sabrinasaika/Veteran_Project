Introduction 
The three arbitrators met to deliberate. After deliberation, Arbitrators 
Parker and Norwood could not arrive at an agreement for an arbitration 
award. This arbitrator (Gardner, chair) requested that each of the other 
arbitrators (Diane Parker and Jenna Norwood) submit proposed arbitration 
awards. This arbitrator (Gardner, chair) would then review the proposed 
arbitration awards written by the arbitrators, Parker and Norwood. This 
arbitrator, (Gardner, chair) studied the proposed awards. This arbitrator, 
Gardner (chair), set a date, May 15, 2023, as the deadline for an arbitration 
award for the final split arbitration award. 
This arbitrator (Gardner) requested proposed arbitration awards 
from both arbitrators, Diane Parker and Jenna Norwood, no later than May 
9, 2023. On May 9, 2023 both Arbitrators, Diane Parker and Jenna 
Norwood, sent proposed arbitration awards to this arbitrator (Gardner, 
chair). 
On May 10, 2023 Arbitrator Jenna Norwood sent an amended post 
arbitration award. Arbitrator Norwood wrote as an explanation: “Please 
find attached my amended post arbitration brief for the above. I accidently 
left off a section at the end of section III. Please let me know if you have 
any questions and have a great day.” 
This arbitration award is being sent on or before May 15, 2023.

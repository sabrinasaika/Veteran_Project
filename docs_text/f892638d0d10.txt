Assurances
The State licensing agency (SLA), responsible for administering the Randolph-Sheppard Act program in the State, assures it will (check if yes):
yes	(1) cooperate with the Secretary in applying the requirements of the Randolph-Sheppard Act in a uniform manner when administering the FRRP, pursuant to 34 C.F.R. § 395.3(a)(11)(i), and will comply with all applicable Federal statutory and regulatory requirements for Federal recipients, as well as the terms and conditions of the grant award;
yes    (2) use all funds received under the FRRP in accordance with the terms of the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2021, PL 116-260, Division H, Title III, section 318, specifically for the purposes of:
offsetting losses of blind vendors that occurred during calendar year 2020, to the extent that such losses were not otherwise compensated; and
other purposes authorized under 34 C.F.R. § 395.9 for set-aside funds, but only to the extent any funds remain available after offsetting losses that were incurred by blind vendors during calendar year 2020.
Yes    (3) actively participate with the State Elected Committee of Blind Vendors in developing for RSA approval how it will use the FRRP funds, consistent with the information required by Part B of this document, pursuant to 34 C.F.R. §§ 395.3(a)(2), 395.3(a)(6), 395.3(a)(11)(iii), 395.4(a), and 395.14, and Technical Assistance Circular RSA-TAC-21-01. The SLA understands that this information in Part B is required since receipt and use of the FRRP funds represent a change in the SLA’s procedures covering the operation of vending facilities in accordance with Federal laws and its sources of funds for the administration of the program;
yes      (4) expend all FRRP funds, including for administrative purposes, in a manner that is allowable, reasonable, and allocable as required by 2 C.F.R. §§ 200.403 through 200.405;
yes	  (5) not use Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) program funds, either Federal or non-Federal, to cover the administration of the FRRP funds unless such costs are also allowable under the VR program in accordance with section 103(b)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (Rehabilitation Act) and 34 C.F.R. § 361.49(a)(5) (i.e., “financial relief and restoration payments to offset losses of blind vendors that occurred during calendar year 2020” are not allowable expenses under the VR program), and allocable to that program in accordance with the Federal cost principles at 2 C.F.R. §§ 200.403 through 200.405.  In the unusual event an activity under the FRRP funds is also authorized under the VR program and the SLA wants to use VR program funds, either Federal or non-Federal, to pay the administrative costs, the SLA assures it will allocate those costs proportionately across all benefitting programs in accordance with 2 C.F.R. §§ 200.403 through 200.405;
yes	  (6) not use expenditures paid with these FRRP funds to meet the non-Federal share of the VR program, because these are Federal – not non-Federal – funds; and
yes	  (7) satisfy all applicable requirements under the Randolph-Sheppard Vending Facility Program, including those set forth at 34 C.F.R. § 395.3(a)(11) (viii) and (ix), as well as those of 2 C.F.R. part 200, including those governing internal controls (§ 200.302), financial management (§ 200.303), financial reporting (§ 200.328), performance reporting and monitoring (§ 200.329), and grant closeout (§ 200.344).
Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
Based on four meetings with the CBV, the FRRP funds of $566, 572 will be distributed in the following manner:
Losses will be determined by comparing the Set Aside reports of the Net Income 2019 against the Seta Aside reports of the Net Income 2020.
•	95% of the FRRP fund will be obligated to the vendors for their reported loss which calculates at $538,243.40. The BEP will distribute equitably to all 50 qualified blind managers to offset their losses in 2020 in the amount of $10,764.86 or more. ($538,243.40 divided by 50 )
Only compensated income to offset the loss will be the Fair Minimum Return of $1,000 per vendor that was distributed in 2020.
Blind managers who incurred less than $10,764.86 in 2020 will be reimbursed for their total uncompensated losses and the remaining funds will be evenly distributed to the other blind managers.    No manager will receive any compensation greater than their actual loss.
For example, a manager who had $5,000 in uncompensated losses would receive $5,000 in FRRP funds and the remaining $5,764.86 would be evenly distributed to the other managers incurred loss of more than $10,764.86.
Based upon the document (attached Assurance Letter) submitted by all the vendors, calculation are as follows:
Four managers reported no losses
Six reported losses less than $10,764.86
The remaining balance from these ten managers equal to $62,107.98. This was applied to the other 40 managers to bring their total compensation to $12,317.56
Six reported losses less than $12,317.56
The remaining balance from these six managers equal to $5300.78.
This was applied to the other 34 remaining managers to bring their total compensation to $12,473.48.
Total obligated compensation to the vendors will be $ $538,243.52.
There is .12 cent over the amount and this will be taken out of the administrative fee below.
•	5% , -$28,328.6, will be utilized for administrative, accounting/bookkeeping needs in regards to the administration and distribution of the FRRP funds.

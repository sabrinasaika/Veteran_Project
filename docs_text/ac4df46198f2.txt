RE: Federal Fiscal Year 2023 Maintenance of Effort Deficit (Revision) 
 
Dear Samantha Fenderson and Elissa Rowe: 
 
On May 7, 2025, the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) issued a letter to the State of 
Maine (Maine) regarding it not satisfying the maintenance of effort (MOE) requirement under 
the State Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VR) program during Federal fiscal year (FFY) 
2023 and, as a result, of the need to reduce the Maine Bureau of Rehabilitation Services, 
Division of Vocational Rehabilitation (DVR) grant for FFY 2026 by $582,656.00, the amount of 
the MOE shortfall. RSA calculated the MOE shortfall based on information contained in the 
Federal Financial Reports (RSA-17) submitted by DVR and the Division for the Blind and 
Visually Impaired (DBVI) at the time the letter was issued. 
 
After receiving RSA’s letter, DVR notified RSA that the 4
th quarter and final RSA-17 reports for 
the FFY 2021 VR award included an error in which non-Federal share had been incorrectly 
reported. DVR’s internal controls had failed to identify the error. DVR requested the ability to 
revise the reported non-Federal share and provided documentation from the Maine State system 
of accounting that RSA reviewed and found to support the accuracy of DVR’s requested 
revisions. DVR corrected the reporting data and RSA recalculated Maine’s FFY 2023 MOE, 
determining that the State had met the requirement. In addition, DVR revised its internal controls 
to safeguard against such inaccurate reporting of non-Federal share in the future. Therefore, there 
will be no reduction to DVR’s FFY 2026 VR award to address a MOE shortfall. See attached 
table titled “Revised MOE Analysis for FFY 2023” for details.

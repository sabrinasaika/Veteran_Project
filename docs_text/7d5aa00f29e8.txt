FINANCIAL RELIEF AND RESTORATION PAYMENTS
AFFIDAVIT
The undersigned Affiant,                                                  , appeared before me, the undersigned witness, and upon oath and affirmation, states the following is true and correct to the best of their knowledge:
I understand that Financial Relief and Restoration Payments (FRRP) appropriations are available to NCBVI-licensed business enterprise vendors who were licensed in 2020 and continue to be licensed, for the purpose of off-setting losses of blind vendors in calendar year 2020.
I was a licensed Business Enterprise vendor in 2020, am currently licensed and had financial losses in 2020 due to circumstances that were not within my control, and for which I have not received compensation.
My net profits after set aside were less in 2020 than in 2019, as indicated by my Profit and Loss Statements, in the amount of _______.
Losses that I incurred in 2020, other than net profits included in my 2020 Profit and Loss Statements, and which were not within my control, exceed the FRRP amount that I will receive based upon a formula created by the Nebraska Commission for the Blind and Visually Impaired in active participation with the Nebraska Blind Vendor Committee (examples, uncompensated spoilage, lost opportunity to expand business or obtain a different facility).
I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true.

7. Explain briefly how the SLA actively participated with the State's Elected Committee of
Blind Vendors to develop the Use of FRRP funds under Part B of this document and the
result of their active participation (e.g., was there consensus on the use of the funds).
Documentation, such as meeting minutes when the use of FRRP funds was discussed,
must be avallable upon request.
The SLA had severalmeetings with the elected blind committee chairperson as well as other
elected blind committee board members to discuss plans, options, ideas, and such. During the
quarterly meeting of the elected committee of blind vendors on March 5,2021, discussions of the
FRRP funds began. On May 14,2021, the elected committee of blind vendors and the SLA
presented and discussed options for the FRRP funds. A unanimous consensus was agreed to and
the elected committee of blind vendors voted and passed this plan after discussions from all
licensees who attended occurred.

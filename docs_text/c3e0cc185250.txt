Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
The funds will be equally shared amongst vendors to offset their cumulative business losses within the pandemic period.
All vendors submit monthly financial statements to BEP office showing gross sales, costs of good, operating expenses and net income. This data is ultimately what the agency uses to build the RSA -15 report each year.
Losses will be determined by comparing Gross Sales and Net Income 2019 against Gross Sales and Net Income 2020 as a group.
Our data shows the group lost
($4,505,091) in lost sales,
($89,200) in lost average income and
($31,581) in lost median income.
Additionally, the same data will reviewed by vendors to determine their individual losses meet criteria for FRRP funds.
This above metric was determined as best industry standard to utilize. It is the key metric being utilized by SBA for PPP and EIDL loans as well as WA State Commerce uses it for their current Cares Act Grants.
To receive funds, vendors will sign an attestation document that losses occurred and other grant/loan funds were disclosed to determine total loss impact.
The distribution of funds is determined to be equal amongst all vendors as individually they each incurred a loss greater than proposed amount of $11,331.47. Break down is
16 shares at $11,331.47 and 1 share at $11,331.48 = $192,635.

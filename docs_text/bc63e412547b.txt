Randolph-Sheppard Financial Relief and Restoration Payments (FRRP)-Wisconsin*
Calculation of BEP losses and distribution of funds plan
Operators who have actual losses in 2020 minus any compensation will be paid with FRRP funds for those losses. Secondly, Operators who had lost income when 2020 is compared to 2019, would be given a proportion of the remaining funds minus any compensation to cover comparable income lost.

The remaining funds will be divided and distributed to cover a portion of  2019-2020 comparable losses. The actual percentage will be determined based on submitted worksheets. It is expected that the 2019-2020 losses (minus compensation) will exceed the remaining FRRP funds. All comparable losses will be tallied, and the amount will be paid proportional to the loss.
STEP 4: Attestation and DOA Vendor Forms
As soon as practical upon approval by RSA of the approval of the plan, each Operator will be sent an email with a worksheet to complete and sign a that confirms the income information from 2019 and 2020 based on submitted profit and loss statements and identifies any eligible compensation received in 2020.
The State of Wisconsin Department of Administration (DOA) requires that each Operator also have a STAR supplier number to receive FRRP funds from the state via check or electronic funds transfer. Those documents will also be sent to the Operators via email with instructions and completed as soon as possible.
Operators will be required to send documentation to the SLA for any compensation received. Documentation will be sent via secure FAX along with the signed worksheet. Operators will also be asked to include a current and secure mailing address for check delivery or to identify if they have elected electronic funds transfer. The Operators will have 10 calendar days to complete and return the worksheet and necessary documentation. Failure to include documentation of compensation, completion of Department of Administration processes or return of the worksheet will result in a delay or relinquishment of payment. The SLA will assist with answering questions.
STEP 5: Payment
Operators with 2020 losses minus compensation will receive payment from the FRRP funds.
Operators with comparable income losses will receive payment from the FRRP funds in proportion to their loss minus any compensation.
DWD Finance will process payments and the DOA will issue checks or electronic funds transfer payments and DOA will issue 1099 forms to the Operators earning more than $600.00.
The SLA has until September 30, 2021 to distribute the funds.
*The Elected Committee voted to approve this plan on June 14, 2021. The plan will be marked as draft until final approval by the Rehabilitation Services Administration.

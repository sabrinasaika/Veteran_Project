Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
NH plans on using a formula introduced by the Elected Committee of Blind Vendors that they used when applying for NH Grant funds. Working with the committee the NH SLA created a spreadsheet using profit and loss reports to document 2019 Adjusted Gross Income then subtracted 2020 AGI. From this amount, any form of compensation received was deducted so that there would not be a duplication of compensation. This included NH Grant relief monies, employment compensation, product spoilage compensation and any other grant monies received. Emergency or PPP loans that are required to be paid back were not used as a deduction.
Using this formula 2 managers showed profit in 2020 so they would not be eligible for FRRP monies. The remaining 5 managers demonstrated a loss of revenues, 3 of the managers with smaller operations can be provided with full reimbursement for the loss they experienced, the remaining amount would be divided equally for the 2 remaining managers with larger business operations. There are not enough funds to compensate them fully but they would be receiving a significant amount. The committee agreed not to divide the monies evenly as they saw it as not being an equitable method for NH to use.

Explain briefly how the SLA actively participated with the State’s Elected Committee of Blind Vendors to develop the Use of FRRP funds under Part B of this document and the result of their active participation (e.g., was there consensus on the use of the funds).  Documentation, such as meeting minutes when the use of FRRP funds was discussed, must be available upon request.
The Elected Committee of Blind Vendors met in person on 3/11/21 for their quarterly meeting, FRRP funds were discussed; meeting minutes can be provided upon request. The committee unanimously agreed to use a NH State grant formula they had experienced, expressing it would be an equitable approach while adhering to the guidelines from RSA to: 1.) not compensate for an area that was already compensated for, 2.) not compensate for a manager that made a greater profit in 2020 than they did in 2019. A form has been provided for each manager to complete to document their eligibility for disbursement of FRRP monies (attachment 1).
The SLA has had ongoing meetings in person once a month with the Elected Committee chair in Jan. and Feb. 2021, to keep him informed on updates received from RSA in regards to FRRP monies.

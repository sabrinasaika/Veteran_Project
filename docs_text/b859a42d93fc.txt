ATTACHMENT A
REHABILITATION SERVICES ADMINISTRATION
Randolph-Sheppard Financial Relief and Restoration Payments (FRRP) Appropriation
Use of FRRP Funds
The West Virginia Division of Rehabilitation Services (WVDRS), as SLA for the Randolph Sheppard Program will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year. The State allotment will be divided among the seven (7) active vendors.  Under this option, six vendors will receive payments in the amount of $25,900.43 with one vendor receiving $25,900.42 (one penny less).  The Blind Vendor Committee Chair has agreed to receive one penny less which makes the amount $25,900.42 and brings the total amount of the distribution to equal the state allotted amount of $181,303.
The SLA, with the Active Participation and collaboration of the West Virginia Committee of Blind Vendors have mutually chosen the following formula to determine loss:
A comparative of financial data from calendar year 2019 and calendar year 2020, and the estimated anticipated losses from the vendors inability to operate open locations that were closed due to the COVID-19 pandemic.  Locations that could have been awarded to blind vendors were not open to provide income during the pandemic.  This loss directly affected the possibility of earnings during the 2020 calendar year. This applies not only to new vendors who cannot show losses for 2019 due to their entry into the program but current vendors who were unable to access an awarded facility in 2020.  The SLA will use either historical data or projection losses based upon facilities of similar population and numbers of machines within facility.
The SLA will use additional data, to determine what losses were compensated (e.g.: Vendors who were able to have their inventory bought back by the SLA, etc.) for each of the 7 vendors, if applicable. If not, then uncompensated loss of product can be added as per RSA FAQ 21-04, answer to question #3. This assures vendors either were compensated for their inventory in calendar year 2020 or compensated via the FAQ from the funds provided.  If losses incurred exceed the maximum amount to award, the vendor receives that amount, if not, then the vendor will receive up to his or her maximum loss based on the agreed upon methodology.
The SLA and Committee of Blind Vendors mutually agree to only count inventory buyback in 2020 as “otherwise compensated”. We mutually agree nothing else listed as per RSA 21-04, question 5 is being used to determine "otherwise compensated'."
The SLA actively participated with the Committee of Blind Vendors to develop the Use of FRRP funds. Emails were exchanged between the Executive Chair with authorization if the Committee and the Randolph Sheppard Manager on February 10, 15, 16, 19, 26 March 1, 9, 16, 23, April 1, 2, 6, 9 and 13th ,2021. One conference call was engaged on February 26, 2021 between the Program Manager and the WVCBV, the second between the Executive Chair, Program Manager and Senior Manager, Blind Services, WVDRS. Documentation, such as emails and meeting minutes will be made available upon request.
The SLA anticipates obligation of all FRRP funds by September 30, 2021 and will assure all funds are obligated by September 30, 2022 in accordance with the guidelines. Vendors will receive funds either via direct deposit to their bank account (vendors may need to supply information to allow for this process to occur, such as a voided check) or paper check mailed to the address the vendor has on file with the SLA.

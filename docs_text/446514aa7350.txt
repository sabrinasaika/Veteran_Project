=== SHEET: Intro ===
VR Pre-Employment Transition Service Reserve Calculator Tool 										
Purpose:  This calculator serves as a tool for State VR agencies to calculate the estimated amount of funds required to be reserved by a State for pre-employment transition services, based on non-Federal and Federal fund variables, to plan for and assess the progress of a single State VR agency or a State with two VR agencies toward meeting the reserve requirement.										
General Instructions: There are two additional spreadsheets in this workbook. The first is for States having one VR agency (Combined). The second is for States with two VR agencies (General and Blind). To utilize the calculators, the following fiscal data is needed: 1) total Federal VR award allotment for one or both VR agencies, depending on State structure; and 2) total non-Federal share provided by one or both VR agencies, depending on State structure. If you have questions about the calculator or how the reservation requirement is calculated, contact the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) Financial Management Specialist assigned to your agency.										
Acknowledgements: This calculator was developed by RSA in collaboration with staff funded by Cooperative Agreement #H326E20003 (NTACT: C) from the Department of Education.

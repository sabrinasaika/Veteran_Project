Strategic Partnership Program
yolBe is proud to partner with select states to help increase their number of clients accessing 
vocational rehabilitation (VR) services while improving employment outcomes. yolBe is a 
mobile-first career growth platform that builds social capital and connections between 
people with disabilities, state VR agencies, employers, and other key constituents. 
The yolBe strategic partnership program is made possible by University of Wisconsin-
Madison and the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality 
Employment (VRTAC-QE).

Mission
Our mission is to dramatically improve the lives of teenagers, young 
adults and working aged people with disabilities everywhere through 
our trusted community by fostering meaningful relationships to build 
their social capital and connecting them to career opportunities like 
jobs, internships, events, trainings, and more.
We intend to be the leading entry-level job and opportunity 
marketplace for the mobile generation.

Why Us?
yolBe creates a trusted community platform for untapped talent to 
connect with career-growing opportunities.  Our innovative technology 
improves the lives of people by helping them build social capital for 
upskilling, jobs, and support services.

Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
There are three blind vendors in the South Dakota Business Enterprise Program.  Two of the three reflected losses for which FRRP funds will be distributed based on the individual vendor’s net profit & loss in 2020 compared to 2019 based on profit and loss statements.  Compensation was included in the information provided by the SLA and vendors will not receive FRRP funds for losses that were previously compensated.   No individual will receive compensation exceeding losses.  The SLA created a spreadsheet using profit & loss statements to determine losses each blind vendor may have sustained in the calendar year 2020.  The spreadsheet was provided to the Committee of Blind Vendors for discussion during the Special Vendor Meetings held to address distribution of FRRP funds.

How will the SLA ensure blind vendor losses were not previously compensated (e.g., attestation, financial reports, inventory reports, documentation of losses due to spoilage, etc.)?
The State of South Dakota has ensured blind vendor losses were not previously compensated by analyzing profit-loss statements, vendor compensation from SD BEP, and the South Dakota Small Business Grant. The State contacted the management services contractor for Profit and Loss information. The the information was tabulated and reviewed by a Senior Accountant and shared with the Blind Vendor Committee.   Unemployment and Paycheck Protection Program payments were not considered in calculating compensation.
An Attestation form was prepared for each vendor who reflected losses to be compensated with FRRP funds. This document represented the actual loss they experienced in 2020 and the amount of compensation they were to receive. Each vendor signed the form to verify the accuracy of the information used to calculate compensation. FRRP compensation payments are contingent upon the Attestation verification.  Vendors will be fully compensated for losses minus other sources prior to any FRRP funds going to Set-Aside.

Explain briefly how the SLA actively participated with the State’s Elected Committee of Blind Vendors to develop the Use of FRRP funds under Part B of this document and the result of their active participation (e.g., was there consensus on the use of the funds).  Documentation, such as meeting minutes when the use of FRRP funds was discussed, must be available upon request.
The State of South Dakota (SLA) first provided a letter of explanation with the award amount on March 1, 2021. Next, the SLA compiled information from profit and loss statements, other compensation and an explanation of the federal requirements related to distribution of FRRP funds to present to the vendor committee via special meetings held specifically to address FRRP fund distribution. In addition to profit and loss statements, the SLA interviewed and requested information from each vendor. Then the SLA presented the information and options for distribution of the FRRP funds to the Vendor Committee on June 9, 2021.  The Vendor Committee voted unanimously to support distribution based on the information provided. Meeting minutes were prepared for the vendors and are available upon request. A second special meeting was held on July 14, 2021 due to a change in compensation for one of the vendors resulting in him no longer being eligible for FRRP funds.  The vendors voted unanimously to support the amended amount resulting in the funds previously identified for the vendor who received compensation going to the set-aside account.
Please identify the date by which the SLA anticipates obligating all FRRP funds, making sure all funds will be obligated by September 30, 2022.
The State of South Dakota intends to obligate the funds by the September 30, 2022 deadline; and will make every effort to obligate by August 31, 2021.

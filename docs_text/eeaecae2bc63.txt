NC BEP Relief Fund Proposal
Total amount of FRRP funds allocated to NC to be distributed is $532,578.
All eligible operators must have a net income loss as documented on the comparison between the 2019 and 2020 calendar year BEP Revenue and Expense Reports.
The total amount of FRRP funds, $532,578, will be divided by the total number of eligible operators to determine the average.  Eligible operators who incurred a net income loss less than the average will receive the amount of FRRP funds equal to their actual loss.
The total amount of the funds to be distributed to operators who incurred a lesser net income loss than the above-referenced average will be deducted from the total FRRP funds allocated.  The balance will be divided by the number of eligible operators remaining to calculate the adjusted average.
Eligible operators who incurred a loss less than this adjusted averaged amount will receive the amount of FRRP funds equal to their actual loss.
The balance will be divided by the number of remaining eligible operators and distributed equally to them.

The Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment (VRTAC-
QE) is seeking proposals, for both concurrent and poster presentations, for its conference being 
held in Madison, WI, from Wednesday, May 22, through Thursday, May 23, 2024. The 
conference theme is, “Partnerships Across Rehabilitation Research, Employment, and 
Empowerment." 
 
The goal of VRTAC-QE is to upgrade and increase the competencies, skills, and knowledge of 
state VR agencies and their affiliates toward increasing their capacity to implement and sustain 
employment strategies and supporting practices that enable individuals with disabilities to 
achieve quality employment and career advancement. In support of this goal, we are looking to 
bring together practitioners, academics, researchers, administrators, and policy makers to 
share contemporary research, issues, and future needs to advance the profession of 
rehabilitation counseling.  
 
The conference will be offered in a hybrid format. Presenters must be available to present their 
work in Madison, WI. Select presentations will be identified by the conference committee for 
national broadcast while others will be provided to in person attendees. As space is limited for 
concurrent presentations, individuals submitting proposals will have the opportunity to accept 
a poster session as an alternative presentation format. Details on the different types of 
presentations are available at the proposal submission portal.  
 
The primary conference track will be vocational rehabilitation interventions and strategies.

Presentations in this track should focus on research or application of methods associated with 
outreach and engagement of individuals with disabilities in vocational rehabilitation services, 
obtain employment as a result of those services, or promote job retention toward career 
establishment and career progression. 
 
Recognizing that readiness to work and sustainability of employment may be affected by 
personal and environmental factors, proposals may also focus on one of the following 
conference tracks: 
• Psychosocial adjustment to disability 
• Collaboration between federal, state, and local partners 
• Health and wellness of persons with disabilities 
• Community living 
• Pre-service or in-service education of rehabilitation counselors 
• Advanced research methods

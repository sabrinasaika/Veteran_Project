B.  Use of FRRP Funds 
1. Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset 
losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based 
on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.). 
 
Profit and Loss statements from calendar year 2019 were compared to those of 
2020.  All vendors who incurred a loss not previously compensated of $88,225 will 
be compensated at 100% of their losses, there were six vendors in this category.  All 
vendors who incurred a loss not previously compensated greater than $88,225 will 
be compensated at $88,225, there were 2 vendors in this category.  Two vendors did 
not incur financial losses.  (Please refer to attached spreadsheet).

7. Explain briefly how the SLA actively participated with the State’s Elected Committee of 
Blind Vendors to develop the Use of FRRP funds under Part B of this document and the 
result of their active participation (e.g., was there consensus on the use of the funds).  
Documentation, such as meeting minutes when the use of FRRP funds was discussed, 
must be available upon request.  
 
Discussion began with the SLA and 2 committee vendor members.  The original 
plan was discussed, and we agreed to present it at the Nevada Committee of Blind 
Vendors on May 13, 2021.  The plan was discussed in detail including what would be 
considered as “otherwise compensated”.  All vendors were given 30 days to present 
questions, comments, or concerns to the committee and/or SLA.  Based on this 
feedback 2 plans were presented at the next Nevada Committee of Blind Vendors 
meeting on June 15, 2021.  The committee board members voted unanimously to 
accept the original plan. 
 
 
8. Please identify the date by which the SLA anticipates obligating all FRRP funds, making 
sure all funds will be obligated by September 30, 2022.  
 
Our plan is to distribute all funds to vendors within 30 days after funds are received 
from RSA.

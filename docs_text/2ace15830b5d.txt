California Department of Rehabilitation, Business Enterprises Program
Vendor Attestation
Randolph-Sheppard Financial Relief and Restoration Payments
Declaration of Losses in 2020 and Certification of Compensation Received
As part of the Randolph-Sheppard Financial Relief and Restoration Payments (FRRP) Appropriation, the California Department of Rehabilitation (DOR) Business Enterprises Program (BEP) is required by the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) to:
Cooperate with the Secretary in applying the requirements of the Randolph-Sheppard Act in a uniform manner when administering the FRRP, pursuant to 34 C.F.R. § 395.3(a)(11)(i), and will comply with all applicable Federal statutory and regulatory requirements for Federal recipients, as well as the terms and conditions of the grant award; and
Use all funds received under the FRRP in accordance with the terms of the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2021, PL 116-260, Division H, Title III, section 318, specifically for the purposes of:
offsetting losses of blind vendors that occurred during calendar year 2020, to the extent that such losses were not otherwise compensated; and
Actively participate with the State Elected Committee of Blind Vendors in developing for RSA approval how it will use the FRRP funds, consistent with the terms of the Consolidated Appropriations Act. This form documents individual California BEP vendors losses for calendar year 2020 and any Fair Minimum Returns (FMR), or other compensation received.
On page 2, DOR has filled in 2019 and 2020 net earnings from your Monthly Operating Reports, FMR amount issued to you, and liability insurance payments paid through DOR for 2020 losses. Please indicate other compensation (if any) received for calendar year 2020 losses. This amount will be included in the calculation to determine your total uncompensated losses for 2020.
It is each vendor’s responsibility to verify the amounts reported and identify any other compensation received for 2020 business losses.
The DOR anticipates distribution of the FRRP funds within 90 days of receipt of FRRP funds from RSA and will assure all funds are obligated by September 30, 2022 in accordance with required RSA assurances. Eligible vendors will receive funds by check mailed to the address the vendor has on file with BEP.

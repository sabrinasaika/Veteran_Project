About this Event:
Join us for a discussion on how the Illinois Model can be used to guide case conceptualization and facilitate vocational rehabilitation service delivery in the State/Federal Vocational Rehabilitation system. The presentation will focus on developing a comprehensive and integrated view of the consumer that can be used to identify appropriate vocational strategies and interventions that leverage consumer strengths and address consumers limitations to facilitate competitive integrated employment.
Learning Objectives:
Identify the domains and factors associated with the Illinois Model.
Apply the Illinois Model to identify consumer strengths and limitations and identify appropriate vocational interventions that facilitate competitive integrated employment.

Meet Our Presenter!
David Strauser
,
Ph.D., CRC
Professor
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
David Strauser is a professor at Illinois in the College of Applied Health Sciences. He specializes in the interaction between the physical and psychological functioning on the career development process and labor market participation of young adults. Additionally, his research interests include examining the impact of contextual factors such as poverty, employer perspectives, family, and the labor market on the overall employment and labor market participation of people with chronic health conditions.

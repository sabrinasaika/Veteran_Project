This Outreach Guide presents solutions and strategies to enhance vocational rehabilitation outreach. Existing barriers may impede access to vocational rehabilitation, however there are strategies that can mitigate these barriers. Outreach strategies provide avenues to communities otherwise missed and provide ways for vocational rehabilitation to interact with new communities. This guide assists agencies with finding those avenues and better meeting the needs of these communities.
Find Example Materials
Examples of outreach materials are provided. These materials can be edited and modified for use by local agencies. These materials are available for distribution to high school students, young people, business owners, and the general public for the purpose of publicizing vocational rehabilitation services and contact information.
Explore Resources
These resources outline specific collaborative contacts and organizations to engage with various communities, as well as providing data resources for outreach evaluation and materials for electronic and physical mailings and posters.

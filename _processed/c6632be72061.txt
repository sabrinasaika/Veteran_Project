In this toolkit, we explore the advantages of implementing Career Pathways for individuals with disabilities, the tools available to VR agencies and their staff to facilitate this transformation, and the collaboration with external partners to ensure successful outcomes.
Career pathways offer a dynamic approach to vocational rehabilitation, aiming to foster lifelong career development and successful engagement in the modern workforce for individuals with disabilities. This comprehensive model seeks to stimulate and support systems change within Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) agencies, aligning their programs, policies, procedures, and practices with Career Pathways initiatives.
By embracing Career Pathways, we aim to improve outcomes for persons with disabilities while meeting the needs of local businesses and creating a more inclusive and vibrant workforce.

More about this Toolkit:
Who is this for?
This Career Pathways Toolkit was created for Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors, Community Resource Providers, consumers, and other stakeholders. Some resources are meant as introductory materials for those who are new to the Career Pathways model and other materials are practical tools to assist with implementation.
How was the toolkit created?
This Toolkit was developed through the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment (VRTAC-QE). It was created by a Learning Community on Career Pathways composed of State Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors, Program Managers, Training & Development Coordinators, Community Resource Providers, and Researchers.

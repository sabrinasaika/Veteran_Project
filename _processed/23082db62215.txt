BE operators will be compensated for losses that do not exceed actual losses.  See attached.

Explain briefly how the SLA actively participated with the State’s Elected Committee of Blind Vendors to develop the Use of FRRP funds under Part B of this document and the result of their active participation (e.g., was there consensus on the use of the funds).  Documentation, such as meeting minutes when the use of FRRP funds was discussed, must be available upon request. The SLA held conference call meetings with the Elected Committee of Blind Vendors (ECBV) on 3/25, 4/8, 4/16, and 5/3 to share and discuss the Randolph-Sheppard Act Financial Relief and Restoration Payments Appropriation, FAQs, and to jointly determine how the funds were to be distributed to eligible operators.  The meetings were recorded.  Questions and concerns were addressed,  and various processes were proposed.  Ultimately, a proposal submitted by the ECBV was agreed upon by the ECBV and the SLA.  Consensus on the use of the funds was established during the meeting on May 3, 2021.  The ECBV chair has provided a statement confirming the ECBV’s active participation in the development of the proposal on the attached document.
Please identify the date by which the SLA anticipates obligating all FRRP funds, making sure all funds will be obligated by September 30, 2022.  The SLA will obligate all FRRP funds by September 30, 2022.

About this Event:
Review the results of a recent needs assessment focused on transportation for individuals with disabilities.
The webinar will provide background information about the requirement for accessible public transportation for people with disabilities. It will also highlight specific barriers experienced by individuals with disabilities, along with potential strategies for improvement.
Participants will obtain knowledge of the most significant barriers to public transportation and develop ideas to advocate for people with disabilities to improve transportation systems and services, which will promote employment opportunities for individuals with disabilities.

Meet Our Presenter!
Jill Bezyak
,
Ph.D.
Professor in Rehabilitation Counseling and Sciences
University of Northern Colorado
Dr. Jill Bezyak is a Professor in Rehabilitation Counseling and Sciences at the University of Northern Colorado. She also serves as the Principal Investigator to the Rocky Mountain ADA Center. Dr. Bezyak is also a Licensed Psychologist and Certified Rehabilitation Counselor with clinical experience serving adults with severe mental illness, substance use disorder, and chronic illness. Dr. Bezyak’s areas of research expertise include employment for people with disabilities, implementation of the Americans with Disabilities Act, and full community participation for individuals with disabilities.

B. Use of FRRP Funds 
1. Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset 
losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based 
on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.). 
a. SLA will review the blind vendors gross wages for FY 2019 and FY 2020 to compare 
and review losses. 
b. Will review blind vendors comparable benefits (Guaranteed Fair Minimum, 
Unemployment, SSA benefits) during FY 2020 and what was compensated for spoilage, 
expired, and other losses incurred. 
c. Vendors were compensated with fair minimum return until August 15, 2020. 
d. The FRRP Payments they will receive will offset losses that occurred to the level that 
they were not compensated.  
e. This will allow SLA to ensure blind vendor are being fully compensated before set 
aside is considered.

About this Event:
Join our upcoming webinar designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the diverse strategies employed by State Vocational Rehabilitation Agencies (SVRA) in delivering services to business customers.
This session will delve into the dual customer approach, exploring how SVRA connects with both job seekers and employers at the national, state, and local levels, with a special focus on the National Employment Team (NET).
Additionally, the webinar will offer insights into the strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT) affecting SVRA's business services, equipping attendees with a deeper understanding of the challenges and opportunities in this critical area.

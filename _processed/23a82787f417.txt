B.
Use of FRRP Funds
1. Explain briefly  how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset
losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based
on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
N
orth Dakota currently has only one blind vendor and so the Elected Committee of
Blind Vendors is basically the one individual.  The SLA used the quarterly
income/loss reports from calendar years 2019 and 2020 and compared the total for
the year to determine if there was a loss in 2020 in comparison to 2019.  There was a
loss for the one blind vendor.  The blind vendor also reported lost inventory due to
the COVID shutdown and provided an itemized list of food items that were expired
which is also being included in the total loss calculation.  The SLA met with the
Elected Committee of Blind Vendors, presented the information and answered any
questions of the blind vendor.  The Elected Committee was in agreement with the
plan noted above for the total loss calculation.

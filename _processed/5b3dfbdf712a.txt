The Employment and Training Administration recently created a new resource page on WorkforceGPS for helping people who may have recently been resettled to the U.S.
The resources focus on providing training and employment services, specifically to Afghan refugee jobseekers who have recently resettled to the U.S.
In addition, information on people who are from underrepresented populations and those who may use English as a second language is shared.
The page includes a link to
Switchboard, a website with tools and materials, learning opportunities, research, and technical assistance on resettlement-related topics for refugee service providers
.

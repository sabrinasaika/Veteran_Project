Success Story Image
Name
Angel
Body
Angel is a former soccer star who overcame significant challenges, including dyslexia, dyscalculia, and a traumatic brain injury, to achieve her career goals. With support from the
New Mexico Division of Vocational Rehabilitation
, Angel was the first in her family to attend college and she landed her dream job as an assistant athletic trainer for the women’s soccer at the University of Texas-El Paso. As for Angel’s future, she is pursuing a master’s degree in education and would like to take her career to the next level – as an athletic trainer for a professional women soccer players, maybe even for her favorite team. “Ideally, I would love to work for the Portland Thorns. Although my plans have changed through the years, I knew I always wanted to be in soccer and I’m doing it. That’s the coolest thing ever!” Learn more about Angel’s success story
here
.

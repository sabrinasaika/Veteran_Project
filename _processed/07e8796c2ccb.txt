Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) Resources
Overview
President Obama signed
WIOA into law
on July 22, 2014. Title IV of WIOA amended title I of the
Rehabilitation Act of 1973
.
WIOA
is designed to strengthen and improve the nation's public workforce development system by helping Americans with barriers to employment, including individuals with disabilities, achieve high quality careers and helping businesses hire and retain skilled workers.

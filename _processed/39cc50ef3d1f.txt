National Clearinghouse of Rehabilitation Training Materials (NCRTM)
Program Type
: Contract
Program Description
The purpose of the NCRTM is to facilitate the sharing of products developed with Federal discretionary and formula grant funds to support the development of skilled VR personnel. The outcome of the NCRTM is a centralized repository of training and technical assistance material to inform the field of vocational rehabilitation and to effectively prepare vocational rehabilitation counselors and professionals to compete in the global economy. Primary users include RSA staff, VR administrators, RSA stakeholders, Federal partners, RSA formula and discretionary grantees, educators, non-Federal partners, business, VR counselors, interpreter educators, and others.

Accessing Internships and Employment: Insights from College Students and Graduates with Disabilities (RS2)
Presented By:
Jaclyn Camden, M.Ed., NCC, Project Director for Transition RRTC, Assistant Director of CTI, VCU Center on Transition Innovations
Megan Reynolds, OTD, Postsecondary Technical Assistance Associate
Nicole O'Brien, Panelist
A’shauntae Nious, Panelist
Elena Bellinger
Date:
June 26, 2025
View Webcast
Join us as we hear from a panel of three college students or alumni with disabilities about their experiences with finding jobs while enrolled in school. These panelists discuss the supports they received for finding meaningful employment, and the successes and barriers to achieving paid work. Their stories and advice give real-world insight into the experiences of college students with disabilities and demonstrate the impact of stakeholders on the job search.
Panelists:  A’shauntae Nious, Elena Bellinger, and Nicole O’Brien

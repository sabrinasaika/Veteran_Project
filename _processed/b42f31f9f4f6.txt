Various challenges may make the transition from a peer role to a supervisor role uncomfortable and exhausting for the new supervisor and others. While some challenges of this transition are inevitable, these challenges do not have to be unbearable.
This webinar outlines some tips, tools, and resources for effectively transitioning from a peer role to a supervisor role. It will help supervisory professionals successfully transition into their new roles so that they are better prepared to manage high-performing teams.
This training is provided by the
Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Management (VRTAC-QM).

Technical Assistance for State Vocational Rehabilitation Agencies
We will provide technical assistance with the goal of improving the capacity of State Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) agencies to support quality employment outcomes for people with disabilities.
State VR agencies interested in accessing technical assistance can contact us for more information.
The technical assistance provided will also contribute to existing federal, state, and nongovernment initiatives promoting successful implementation of goals and provisions of the Rehabilitation Act introduced by the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA).

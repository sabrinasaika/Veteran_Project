Lucian Friel
Making Resumes Visual: Using Video Resumes in Customized Employment
Presented By:
Lucian Friel, M.S., Education Support Specialist, Virginia Commonwealth University
Date:
July 22, 2020
View Webcast
This webcast covers the topic of video resumes. The structure, style, planning, and editing of video resumes are discussed. Lucian Friel presents information on how to develop a video resume for individuals that can be used with businesses. The focus is on showcasing a job seeker's skills and interests.
Bio:
Lucian is a former Marine journalist and a 2017 graduate of Virginia Commonwealth University. Lucian received his bachelor of science in mass communications with a concentration in public relations and a minor in general business. He joined the RRTC team in January 2018. In addition to providing technical support for webcasts and online courses, Lucian contributes to many project websites, takes photos, and produces videos for RRTC.

Answering the Call with the Support of the VR Program
Wisconsin Division of Vocational Rehabilitation
Success Story Image
Name
Virginia
Body
Virginia successfully managed her anxiety and depression to transition from a high school student facing panic attacks to a professional crisis counselor. With support from the
Wisconsin Department of Workforce Development's Division of Vocational Rehabilitation
, Virginia received funding for college and graduated with a degree in social work. After graduation, she worked with a job developer who helped her secure a position as a crisis counselor with
988 Wisconsin Lifeline
, where she now helps individuals facing their most vulnerable moments. Virginia’s success story illustrates how comprehensive VR services and personal dedication can lead to a fulfilling career and the ability to give back. Learn more about Virginia’s story
here
.

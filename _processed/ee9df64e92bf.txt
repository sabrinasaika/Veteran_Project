We are excited to introduce our new VRTAC-QE to CACREP Crosswalk Tool! This resource is designed to help rehabilitation professionals, counselors, and educators align their practices with the Council for Accreditation of Counseling and Related Educational Programs (CACREP) standards. The crosswalk highlights the connections between the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment (VRTAC-QE) resources and the key domains outlined by CACREP, supporting seamless integration of vocational rehabilitation concepts into accredited counseling programs.
This tool provides a comprehensive view of resources such as training videos, webinars, factsheets, and toolkits that cover critical areas like ethical practices, cultural responsiveness, career development, and specialized vocational rehabilitation services. Whether you're preparing for certification or enhancing your counseling curriculum, this crosswalk ensures you have the guidance and tools needed to meet accreditation standards while delivering high-quality support to individuals with disabilities.
Explore the crosswalk today and streamline your path to enhanced vocational rehabilitation and counseling education!

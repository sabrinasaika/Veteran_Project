TITLE: State Teams | Rehabilitation Services Administration
URL: https://rsa.ed.gov/about/people/state-teams

RSA has four State Teams that support the formula grant programs administered by the State Monitoring and Program Improvement Division (SMPID). Each State Team is led by a State Team Facilitator and includes a Financial Management Specialist from the Fiscal Unit along with SMPID staff from the VR Program Unit, Technical Assistance Unit, and Data Collection and Analysis Unit.

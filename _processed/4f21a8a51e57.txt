The Blindness & Low Vision Certificate Program for Business Development Professionals was developed in response to requests from VR
agencies and is a compilation of five existing NRTC courses, grouped together to provide foundational information specifically for business development professionals who do not have experience working with individuals with blindness and low vision. This program includes information from the following NRTC courses:
Introduction to Blindness and Low Vision
Basics of Blindness and Low Vision
Improving Business Development
Overview of AT for Individuals Who are Blind or Visually Impaired
AT for Individuals Who Are Blind or Visually Impaired in the Workplace
Developed by the
National Research and Training Center on Blindness and Low Vision
(NRTC) at Mississippi State University.

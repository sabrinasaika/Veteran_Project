By Us and For Us: Legacy and Future of Our Movement
Date:
Tuesday, April 15 - Wednesday, April 16, 2024
Location:
Honolulu, Hawai’i
2025 is the 40th anniversary of the Pacific Rim International Conference on Disability. We invite you to Honolulu on April 15th and 16th to celebrate.
Come join us both to honor the past and to consider the future of the global movement we create together. Letʻs all recognize our achievements, share our struggles, and develop strategies for the work that remains. See you in Honolulu in 2025!
Pac Rim 2025 is hosted by the
Center on Disability Studies
(CDS), College of Education, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

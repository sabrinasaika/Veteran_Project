Everyday Ethics in VR Practice: Monthly Seminars for Agency Staff (Ethics)
About this Session
This training explores the practical application of ethics in everyday vocational rehabilitation (VR) practice, emphasizing the importance of continuous discussion and reflection on ethical decision-making. Participants will learn about boundary challenges, cultural considerations, and professional responsibilities in various settings, including rural and culturally specific communities. Through real-world scenarios, this session highlights the nuanced nature of ethical dilemmas and the importance of engaging in open, inclusive conversations to foster ethical awareness across all roles within VR agencies.
Learning Objectives:
Understand how to navigate boundary challenges and ethical dilemmas
in diverse settings, including rural and culturally specific communities, to promote ethical and inclusive VR practices.
Learn strategies for fostering open dialogue and continuous reflection on ethical issues
within VR agencies, encouraging all staff to engage in shared responsibility for ethical decision-making.

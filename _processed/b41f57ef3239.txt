We are excited to invite you to join one of our Toolkit Implementation Groups. This is a unique opportunity under the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment (VRTAC-QE). Each group is designed to bring together a diverse set of professionals from state vocational rehabilitation (VR) agencies to collaboratively implement a comprehensive Toolkit developed by TACQE learning communities.
By participating, you will contribute to a transformative effort aimed at enhancing employment outcomes for individuals with disabilities. Your expertise and commitment will be essential as we work together to drive meaningful, long-term change within the VR community.

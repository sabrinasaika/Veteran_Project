State Monitoring and Program Improvement Division
Christopher Pope, Director
Christopher Pope has served as Director of the State Monitoring and Program Improvement Division since 2024. From 2019 to 2024, Mr. Pope served as Chief of the Data Collection and Analysis Unit. From 2016 to 2019, he coordinated the Federal interagency implementation of the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act. Mr. Pope joined RSA as a Vocational Rehabilitation Program Specialist within the VR Program Unit in 2012. Prior to RSA, Mr. Pope served as a Rehabilitation Specialist at the U.S. Department of Labor and began his career as a Rehabilitation Counselor with the New York State Education Department. Mr. Pope holds a Master of Arts in Rehabilitation Counseling from The George Washington University and he is a Certified Rehabilitation Counselor.
Michael Quinn, Chief: Data Collection and Analysis Unit
Michael Quinn has served as Chief of the Data Collection and Analysis Unit since 2024. From 2021 to 2024, Mr. Quinn served as a Management and Program Analyst within the Data Collection and Analysis Unit. Prior to joining RSA, Mr. Quinn had a 22 year career with the Alabama Department of Rehabilitation Services, serving as the agency’s Program Evaluation Coordinator from 2009 to 2021. Mr. Quinn holds a Master of Health Science in Rehabilitation Counseling from Louisiana State University.
David Steele, Chief: Fiscal Unit
David Steele has served as Chief of the Fiscal Unit since 2011. From 2008 to 2011, Mr. Steele served as a Financial Management Specialist within the Fiscal Unit that he now leads. From 2005 to 2008, he served as an Education Program Specialist with the Office of Special Education Program's Monitoring and State Improvement Planning Division. Prior to Federal service, during his 16 years as an employee of the State of South Carolina, Mr. Steele worked in several different public service roles, including IDEA Part C Coordinator. Mr. Steele earned a Master of Arts in Public Administration and a Master of Social Work, with a specialization in administration, from The Ohio State University.
Ed Vitelli, Chief: Technical Assistance Unit
Ed Vitelli has served as Chief of the Technical Assistance Unit since 2022 and Acting Chief of the VR Program Unit since 2025. Prior to joining RSA, Mr. Vitelli served as a Group Leader in the U.S. Department of Education's Office of Elementary and Secondary Education, which administers the Equity Assistance Centers program, the Student Engagement and Attendance Center, and the Comprehensive Centers program. From 2011 to 2013 and again from 2014 to 2019, he served in the Office of the Assistant Secretary in the Office of Special Education and Rehabilitative Services. Prior to Federal service, Mr. Vitelli worked as a Project Director and Lecturer at The George Washington University and as a Special Education Teacher in D.C. Public Schools. He earned a doctorate in special education from The George Washington University.
Training and Service Programs Division
Ashley Brizzo, Director
Ashley Brizzo has served as Director of the Training and Service Programs Division since 2024 and Acting Chief of the Training Programs Unit since 2025. From 2019 to 2024, Ms. Brizzo served as a Group Leader in the U.S. Department of Education’s Office of Elementary and Secondary Education, which administers the Education Innovation and Research program. From 2015 to 2019, she worked in the Office of State Support administering various state formula grant programs under the Every Student Succeeds Act. Prior to Federal service, Ms. Brizzo worked as an Evaluator and Technical Assistance Provider; she also taught special education in South Florida. Ms. Brizzo has Bachelor and Master of Science degrees in special education and earned a doctorate in education policy from The George Washington University.
Corinne Weidenthal, Chief: Service Programs Unit
Corinne Weidenthal has served as Chief of the Service Programs Unit since 2019. From 2006 to 2019, Ms. Weidenthal worked in the Office of Special Education Programs within OSERS. Prior to Federal service, she served as a Project Director for a doctoral leadership program in special education at The George Washington University, where she received a doctorate in the same program. Ms. Weidenthal has also worked as a Special Education Teacher in U.S. Department of Defense schools internationally and in the U.S.

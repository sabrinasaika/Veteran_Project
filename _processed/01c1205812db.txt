Project E3, the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Targeted Communities, worked with state vocational rehabilitation agencies and their partners across the United States to help people with disabilities from all communities achieve their independent living and employment goals.
Watch these videos to learn how participating state agencies and their community partners planned to sustain their improved services and positive employment outcomes after project end:

Legislation
RSA administers legislation that directly influences the lives of individuals with disabilities and their families. RSA plays critical roles in both the development of Federal regulations that bring legislation into effect and in monitoring that legislation and Federal regulations are followed.
The Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended by title IV of the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA)
President Obama signed
WIOA into law
on July 22, 2014. This legislation and its implementing Federal regulations are designed to strengthen and improve our nation's public workforce system and help Americans with barriers to employment, including individuals with disabilities, into high quality careers and help employers hire and retain skilled workers.

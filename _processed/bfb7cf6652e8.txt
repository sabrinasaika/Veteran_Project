Geoff Loughrey
Training & Technical Assistance Associate
loughreygs@vcu.edu
Geoff Loughrey, BCBA, LBA, has worked in the field of applied behavior analysis for over 10 years and worked with individuals with Autism in various capacities for 17 years. His initial experience started at a Severe Behavior and Feeding Research clinic affiliated with Upstate Medical University. Since then, Geoff has worked in a variety of settings including: Private Day Schools, Adult Specific Settings, Home-Based Therapy, and Center-Based Therapy. Geoff has extensive experience in clinical training, professional development/coaching, leadership training, and organizational analytics. Clinically, severe behaviors and trauma informed approaches are of specialized experience and interest. He is most passionate about providing quality supervision to aspiring behavior analysts.

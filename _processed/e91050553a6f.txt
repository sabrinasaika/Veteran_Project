Rural Outreach: Idaho Demographics Part 1 - Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment
▸
About this training
This presentation examines Idaho's demographics to answer the following questions:
Where are the population concentrations in Idaho?
How does income differ across the state?
Where are unemployment figures highest?
What region faces the deepest economic disadvantages?
Where are poverty rates higher than other counties?
Where do Hispanic, Latino, or Mexican-born Idahoans live?
What areas in Idaho are home to Native Americans?
Are veterans dispersed all over Idaho, or are they more likely to live in certain areas?
In addition, we'll examine Idaho's disability figures.

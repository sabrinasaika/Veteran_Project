Join this webinar to learn about the Project E3 four phases of intensive technical assistance applied by the VR agencies of California, Kentucky, Louisiana, New Jersey, New Mexico, North Carolina, Oregon, and Virginia. The goal was to increase vocational rehabilitation participation and greater quality employment outcomes for people with disabilities in traditionally marginalized communities populations in their States. Also, learn about the targeted technical assistance provided by Project E3 in Wisconsin.

Training and technical assistance is now available from the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Management (VRTAC-QM)!
VRTAC-QM provides training and technical assistance that enables State VR agency personnel manage available resources, improve effective service delivery, and increase the number and quality of employment outcomes for people with disabilities. Their areas of expertise include:
VR program and performance quality management
Fiscal and resource quality management of the VR program
General quality management of organizations
Visit VRTAC-QM
VRTAC-QM Featured Resource: General Quality Management of Organizations
Tap into VRTAC-QM’s collection of resources to assist VR professionals in their overall supervision and management practices. Resources include in-depth information on popular Quality Management Systems used in industry, resources for managing organizations by distance, and various learning resources regarding the leadership of organizations.

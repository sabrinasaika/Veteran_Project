RE: Federal Fiscal Year 2023 Maintenance of Effort Deficit 
 
Dear Linda Schramm and DaMia Harris-Madden: 
 
On May 13, 2025, the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) issued a letter to the State 
of New York (New York) regarding it not satisfying the maintenance of effort (MOE) 
requirement under the State Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VR) program during Federal 
fiscal year (FFY) 2023 and, as a result, of the need to reduce the New York State Department of 
Education’s (NYSED’s) grant for FFY 2026 by $5,779,070, the amount of the MOE shortfall. 
RSA calculated the MOE shortfall based on information contained in the Federal Financial 
Reports (RSA-17) submitted by NYSED and the New York Office of Children & Family 
Services at the time the letter was issued. 
 
After receiving RSA’s letter, NYSED notified RSA that the final RSA-17 report for FFY 2023 
contained errors and omissions and questioned whether there was a MOE deficit. NYSED’s 
internal controls failed to identify inaccuracies and omissions in the RSA-17 report submitted in 
the RSA Management Information System. NYSED’s internal controls for financial reports 
should include a validation and/or reconciliation component to ensure there are no similar issues 
in the future.  
 
NYSED requested to revise its FFY 2023 final financial report. After review of the proposed 
changes by the RSA Financial Management Specialist, the form was opened to permit edits in 
the RSA Management Information System. Upon receipt of the revised data, RSA recalculated 
New York’s FFY 2023 MOE and determined that the State had met the requirement. Therefore, 
there will be no reduction to NYSED’s FFY 2026 VR award to address a FFY 2023 MOE 
deficit. See attached table titled “Revised MOE Analysis for FFY 2023” for details.

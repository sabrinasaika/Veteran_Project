Thank you for your interest in the Veterans Employment Outreach Learning Community.  At this time, application to this group is now closed.  If you submitted an interest application earlier, stay tuned for more information soon.  We will contact you by email with more information soon.

Thank you for your interest in the Veterans Employment Outreach Learning Community.
Applicants will be chosen to form a diverse group including counselors, supervisors, district managers, field service directors, community colleges, businesses, and persons with disabilities across the country. Participants in the community will work together on developing a
Veterans Employment Outreach Toolkit
for national distribution.

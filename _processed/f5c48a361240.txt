Florida BBE Application and Attestation for FRRP
Explanation and purpose of the survey
FRRP are to only be used to offset financial losses of blind vendors during the calendar year of 2020.
Reported losses must be verifiable and not have been otherwise compensated. The SLA and
Committee of Vendors have agreed to determine vendor losses based on the net profit reported by
vendors on the MBR from March - December 2020 in comparison to March December of 2019. The SLA
and Committee of Vendors will distribute FRRP funds in accordance with the guidelines set forth by
the RSA. 
The purpose of the survey is to collect information on compensation. Failure to complete the survey
could result in a delay or forfeiture of your award.

Thank you for your interest in the Subminimum Wage Learning Community.
Applicants will be chosen to form a diverse group including counselors, supervisors, district managers, field service directors, community colleges, businesses, and persons with disabilities across the country. Participants in the community will work together on developing a
Subminimum Wage Toolkit
for national distribution.

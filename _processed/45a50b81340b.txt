Strategies and resources are available from the Job Accommodation Network (JAN) to help employers and employees with disabilities navigate the COVID-19 job accommodations process.
JAN’s practical job accommodation strategies can enable workers with disabilities to return to the work environment, work at home, or access leave when other accommodations are not reasonable. Resource topics include:
Vaccinations and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
COVID-19 long haulers and the ADA
Accommodating employees with COVID-related symptoms
Requesting and negotiating accommodations during the pandemic
Teleconference accessibility and hearing — Keeping deaf and hard of hearing employees in the loop
Leave and unemployment related resources
And more
Accommodation and Compliance: COVID-19
Note:
JAN does not provide public health, safety, or legal advice, but does offer Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) compliance assistance and practical job accommodation strategies for returning individuals with disabilities to work during the COVID-19 pandemic.

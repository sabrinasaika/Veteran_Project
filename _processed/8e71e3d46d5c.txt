National Trends in Disability Employment (nTIDE) – issued semi-monthly by Kessler Foundation and the University of New Hampshire
East Hanover, NJ – December 3, 2021 – November marked the fourth consecutive month of employment gains for people with disabilities, according to today’s National Trends in Disability Employment – Monthly Update (nTIDE), issued by
Kessler Foundation
and the
University of New Hampshire’s Institute on Disability
(UNH-IOD). This four-month increase is in stark contrast to the declining trend for people without disabilities.

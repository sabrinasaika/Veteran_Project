TITLE: RSA Commissioner | Rehabilitation Services Administration
URL: https://rsa.ed.gov/about/rsa-commissioner

In accordance with the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 and the Department of Education Organization Act of 1979, the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) is led by a Commissioner who is nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate. The Commissioner advises the Secretary of the U.S. Department of Education and Assistant Secretary for the Office of Special Education and Rehabilitative Services on programs and issues affecting individuals with disabilities.
On March 26, 2025, Secretary McMahon delegated the authority to perform the functions and duties of the Commissioner to Christopher Pope.
Mr. Pope also serves as Director of the State Monitoring and Program Improvement Division, a role he has held since 2024. From 2019 to 2024, Mr. Pope served as Chief of the Data Collection and Analysis Unit. From 2016 to 2019, he coordinated the Federal interagency implementation of the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act on behalf of RSA. Mr. Pope joined RSA as a Vocational Rehabilitation Program Specialist in 2012. Prior to RSA, Mr. Pope served as a Rehabilitation Specialist at the U.S. Department of Labor and began his career as a Rehabilitation Counselor with the New York State Education Department.
Mr. Pope holds a Master of Arts in Rehabilitation Counseling from The George Washington University and he is a Certified Rehabilitation Counselor.

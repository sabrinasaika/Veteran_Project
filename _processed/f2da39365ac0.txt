Over the past five years, TACQE has worked toward one goal: helping vocational rehabilitation (VR) professionals and partners support people with disabilities in finding and keeping quality jobs.
As the project comes to a close in September 2025, that support continues. We're spotlighting
a few of the most-used tools that VR teams turned to time and again
because they get results.
Here's a look at the QE Tools that rose to the top. Keep reading to see why they stood out and how they can keep supporting your work.
The QE Toolkits Collection
A go-to source for practical solutions—shaped by real-world feedback and grounded in implementation science.
These toolkits were created with input from VR professionals just like you—working in state agencies, VA settings, and community-based organizations. Each one helps address a different challenge in supporting people with disabilities on the path to employment.
Explore the toolkit collection
.
Comprehensive Review – VRTAC-QE White Paper
Your research-backed roadmap to better employment outcomes.
This in-depth review highlights proven strategies to boost career readiness and employment success for people with disabilities.
Learn about key findings and recommendations
.
Supported and CE Side-by-Side Referral Decision Guide
Unsure which approach is best for your client? This chart makes it clear.
This easy-to-use tool explains the key differences between supported and customized employment and helps you choose the best fit for each individual’s needs.
Use the decision-making guide
.
VR Outreach Guide
Practical ways to reduce barriers and build stronger connections.
This guide offers actionable strategies to help you reach people with disabilities—including those often overlooked—and better meet their unique needs.
Discover strategies for effective VR outreach
.
Employer Engagement Resource Guide
Your connection point for building strong hiring partnerships.
Help employers understand the value of disability inclusion—and connect them with qualified candidates.
Learn more about connecting employers with qualified candidates
.

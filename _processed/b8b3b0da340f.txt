FFY 2025 Formula Grant Reallotment
Reallotment will be open from Tuesday, July 15, 2025, through Friday, August 15, 2025, for the following programs:
State Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) Services (Assistance Listing Number (ALN) 84.126A).
Client Assistance Program (CAP) (ALN 84.161A);
Independent Living Services for Older Individuals Who Are Blind (OIB) (ALN 84.177B);
State Supported Employment Services (Supported Employment) (ALN 84.187A);
State Supported Employment Services (Supported Employment) (ALN 84.187B); and
Protection and Advocacy of Individual Rights (PAIR) (ALN 84.240A).
RSA anticipates only one FFY 2025 reallotment period.
Grantees must use the Grant Reallotment Form (RSA-692) available through the RSA Management Information System (RSAMIS) to inform RSA of the amount of FFY 2025 grant award funds to be relinquished and/or to request additional funds in the event funds are available for disbursement during the reallotment process.
Only grantees who wish to relinquish or request funds need to submit an RSA-692. Any grantee that does not submit a form by August 15, 2025, will be considered as not relinquishing or requesting funds during the FFY 2025 reallotment period.
For States with two VR agencies, grantees should discuss reallotment preferences to determine whether it may be more efficient to request that RSA transfer funds within the State rather than process changes through reallotment.
Staff submitting the reallotment data must have an RSAMIS account and access to the RSA-692. It is the grantee’s responsibility to ensure access to both the RSAMIS and the RSA-692 prior to the end of the reallotment period. If multiple versions of the form are received from a grantee, by different users, RSA will consider the most recently submitted form to be the final version.
If requesting additional VR, Supported Employment, or OIB funds, the State must provide the required match for the additional funds (i.e., 21.3 percent for VR, 10 percent for Supported Employment funds reserved for youth with the most significant disabilities (ALN 84.187B), and 10 percent for OIB by September 30, 2025). Therefore, grantees should only request additional funds in the amount for which they can provide non-Federal funds for matching purposes by the end of the current FFY. Requesting more funds than a grantee can use, or match will not increase the likelihood of the recipient receiving a different amount.
Funds received during reallotment are an increase to the State’s allotment for the FFY for which funds were appropriated (see Sections 110(b)(3), 112(e)(2), 509(e), and 603(b) of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (Rehabilitation Act), and 34 C.F.R. § 367.32(d)). As such, any funds received or relinquished during reallotment will affect a State’s calculation of the amount of funds to be reserved and expended for the provision of pre-employment transition services under the VR program and the amount to be reserved and expended for the provision of Supported Employment services, including extended services, to youth with the most significant disabilities under the Supported Employment program (ALN 84.187B) (Sections 110(d)(1) and 603(d) of the Rehabilitation Act).
Due to several States not accepting Supported Employment FFY 2025 funds, there will be Supported Employment funds available for reallotment. Because a State’s Supported Employment allotment refers to the total amount of ALN 84.187A and ALN 84.187B funds awarded pursuant to Section 603 of the Rehabilitation Act, the total allotment amount must remain balanced in order to comply with the statutory requirements for a State to reserve and expend funds for the provision of supported employment services for youth with the most significant disabilities and the requirement to provide match for the half of the allotment reserved for youth with the most significant disabilities (50 percent of award in ALN 84.187A and 50 percent in ALN 84.187B). To maintain this balance during the reallotment process, recipients directly enter amounts on the RSA-692 for the Supported Employment-A (ALN 84.187A) award only. The amount of Supported Employment-A funds relinquished or requested will be automatically assigned, in an equal amount, to the Supported Employment-B (ALN 84.187B) program. For example, if a grantee enters a relinquishment amount of $100,000 in Supported Employment-A funds, the RSA-692 for the Supported Employment-B award will automatically indicate relinquishment of $100,000.
It is important that grantees submit the required information by August 15, 2025, so that RSA has time to deobligate relinquished funds and reallot those funds to grantees that can use and match, when required, those funds prior to the end of the FFY. The RSA-692 will not be available for data entry on RSA’s website after August 15, 2025. Therefore, RSA may not be able to process reallotment requests received after that date.
In the event no funds are made available for reallotment in one or more of the programs listed above, RSA will notify grantees that additional funds will not be made available in accordance with the reallotment process for that program(s).
If you have any questions, please contact the RSA Financial Management Specialist for your agency.

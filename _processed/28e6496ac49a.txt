What Employers Should Expect from a Vocational Rehabilitation Program
Presented By:
April Lynch, M.S., CRC, Vocational Rehab Counselor, VCU-RRTC
Date:
May 18, 2020
View Webcast
Are you an employer considering or are ready to partner with a community vocational rehabilitation program and you aren’t sure what to expect?  This webcast will take a closer look at establishing a relationship with Employment Specialists or Vocational Rehabilitation Counselors to explore the process of hiring individuals with disabilities within your business.  How do we go about making a positive job match?  What business tax credits may be involved with hiring an individual with a disability? What is disability awareness training and how could this benefit my staff?  What is job site training and how does that shape employee performance to meet production standards?  Partnering with a VR program is meant to be a valuable experience and promote a diverse workforce!

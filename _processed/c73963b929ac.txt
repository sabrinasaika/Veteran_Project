Vocational Rehabilitation Financial Report (RSA-17) 
SF-425/RSA-2 Data Element Comparison 
While a data element in the SF-425 and RSA-2 may be similar to an RSA-17 data element, this does not mean the data being reported is exactly the same. For 
example, while the chart below lists RSA-2 data elements that are comparable to RSA-17 data elements, the RSA-2 data is submitted annually, includes State 
Supported Employment Service expenditures, and is not specific to a grant award. The RSA-17 data is submitted quarterly, does NOT include State Supported 
Employment Service expenditures, and IS tied to financial activity specific to a grant award. Therefore, agencies should be careful when developing their internal 
controls for the RSA-17 to ensure the correct data is being reported.

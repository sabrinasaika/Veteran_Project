VR Program Monitoring and Technical Assistance
Statutory Authority
Section 107 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended by title IV of WIOA, requires RSA to conduct annual reviews and periodic on-site monitoring of programs authorized under Title I of the Rehabilitation Act to determine whether a VR agency is complying substantially with the provisions of its
WIOA Unified or Combined State Plan
.
Monitoring Reports
Beginning with Federal Fiscal year (FFY) 2007, VR program monitoring reports are searchable by FFY, State, and VR agency type (i.e., Combined, General, or Blind). From FFYs 2007 - 2010, RSA monitored both VR agencies in States that had established two VR agencies; these reports are not searchable by VR agency type because they contain General and Blind VR agency reports.

Topical Index of Monitoring Findings
RSA maintains a
Topical Index of VR Program Monitoring Findings
including findings, observations, and recommendations that RSA has made since FFY 2017. The Index is organized by topics and includes the year monitoring reports were published, links to the monitoring reports, and corresponding page numbers in the reports. RSA updates the Index annually to include findings from the most recent VR program monitoring reports.

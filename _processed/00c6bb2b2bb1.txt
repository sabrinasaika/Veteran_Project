Technology to Power Successful Collaboration in the VOC World
About this Session
This training focuses on how smart technology and artificial intelligence (AI) can enhance remote career guidance services, improving outcomes for individuals with disabilities. Participants will explore how online platforms foster collaboration, build social capital, and offer personalized career support. Practical case studies, such as partnerships with organizations like the Autism Alliance of Michigan, illustrate the benefits of technology-driven solutions in expanding access to employment opportunities and creating inclusive pathways for career growth.
Learning Objectives:
- Understand how smart technology and AI can enhance remote career guidance by fostering collaboration and improving access to employment resources and services.
- Learn strategies for building social capital and engaging with online communities to support individuals with disabilities in navigating career pathways and accessing meaningful employment opportunities.

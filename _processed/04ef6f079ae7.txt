Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
SLA ANSWER:   The New Mexico SLA will distribute the FRRP funds equal to our blind vendor’s individual net profit losses incurred. Our blind vendors will receive full compensation to the extent that such losses were not otherwise compensated and not to exceed their losses. We will review the monthly business reports submitted by each blind vendor for the 12 months in calendar year 2019 against the 12 months in calendar year 2020 to make a comparison and determine the individual net profit losses. In addition to the individual net profit losses on the monthly reports, we will factor in documented spoilage or expired inventory for those vendors that were not able to access their facilities due to building closures.  The amount will then be offset by losses already compensated, such as for PPP Loans, Unemployment Benefits, Fair Minimum Return benefits, and any other grants, if applicable. This will ensure that vendors will receive an equitable benefit of FRRP funds to supplement their actual losses.
Does the SLA anticipate using any of the FRRP funds to cover administrative costs? YES
If yes, what percentage of the FRRP funds do you anticipate expending on administrative costs?  The NM SLA may claim administrative costs up to 10% from the point of award notification to point of grant award.
How will the SLA ensure blind vendor losses were not previously compensated (e.g., attestation, financial reports, inventory reports, documentation of losses due to spoilage, etc.)?
SLA ANSWER:
The SLA already retains records for Fair Minimum Return benefits issued to our blind vendors and will also require documentation from our blind vendors for inventory spoilage and losses already compensated, such as for, PPP Loans, Unemployment Benefits, and any other grants, if applicable.  Additionally, our blind vendors will be required to sign an attestation statement that their individual net profit losses did occur, and all other compensated funds are disclosed. The Attestation Form will list specific examples of benefits that are considered as other compensation and the blind vendor will be required to attest to each item listed as having received or not received compensation in that category as well as the amount they received. Does the SLA anticipate having any FRRP funds remaining after distributing funds to the blind vendors to cover losses incurred in calendar year 2020?  YES, and only after our blind vendors are fully compensated (after other applicable relief funding adjustments).

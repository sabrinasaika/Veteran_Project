Rural and Remote Service Provision in Vocational Rehabilitation Webinar
Join us for a discussion of VR services in rural and remote communities.
Pathway to Trauma-Informed Rehabilitation Counseling Webinar
This webinar presentation provides information on how trauma can significantly impact individuals’ work-related psychosocial functioning, and service engagement, particularly for people with disabilities.
StaffUp ASAP – Accelerated Training for VR Professionals
This webinar will focus on the origins of the StaffUp ASAP project and how it meets the needs of State VR Agencies. We will also introduce our team, provide an overview of the training, and show snippets of the training. The session will wrap up with action steps if attendees would like to learn more or connect with our team.
AccessTECH: Building Strategic Partnerships for VR Participants and Tech Career Advancement
Join us to explore AccessTECH, a comprehensive program designed to empower individuals with disabilities to pursue careers in high-demand technology fields through a structured five-phase process.
Increasing Job Retention Through the Promoting Retention In Meaningful Employment (PRIME) Project
Join us for an insightful webinar exploring strategies to support job retention for individuals with disabilities.
Expanding Access: Leveraging Strategic Partnerships and Funding toward Transportation Options
Accessible and reliable transportation is essential for community inclusion, workforce participation, and independent living. This webinar will explore innovative solutions to fill transportation gaps for individuals with disabilities and older adults.
Maximizing Vocational Rehabilitation Outcomes: Leveraging High Quality Technical Assistance for Lasting Impact
This webinar will highlight the value of technical assistance centers in strengthening vocational rehabilitation services. Participants will gain insight from lessons learned by states engaged in intensive technical assistance (ITA) and explore the pathway from receiving support to successfully implementing strategies.

About this Event:
Join us to explore the transformative impact of building social connections on the career trajectories of individuals with disabilities. We will uncover the role of new generative AI tools in enhancing accessibility and support within career platforms, particularly for those with disabilities. Additionally, participants will delve into how advanced technology, such as assistive AT and innovative virtual platform features, fosters collaboration among vocational rehabilitation stakeholders, ultimately enhancing service delivery for clients with disabilities.
Learning Objectives:
Learn how building social capital benefits people with disabilities, and how it helps improve their careers.
Learn how new generative AI tools play an essential role in a career platform and how they can be a benefit to people with disabilities in their career journey.
Learn how advanced technology including assistive AT, and other innovative features of a modern virtual platform can improve collaboration between vocational rehabilitation partners and vendors to better serve their clients.

Meet Our Presenters!
Andree Dolan
Director of Strategic Initiatives
yolBe Inc.
Andree Dolan is Director of Strategic Initiatives for Yolobe, Inc. As a senior business strategy, sales, and marketing executive primarily in the technology industry, Ms. Dolan brings more than two decades of experience in helping businesses, nonprofits, and entrepreneurs execute their strategic goals and communications plans, establish new programs, and increase customer and partner engagement. Ms. Dolan has deep experience in private, public, and nonprofit sectors, and brings a unique combination of expertise in technology, product development, marketing, DEI initiatives, and disability inclusion.
At Yolobe, Ms. Dolan leads strategic business development to establish, manage, and grow critical partner initiatives. As a mobile-first, purpose-driven community platform and app, yolBe is focused on helping young adults create a trusted professional social network where untapped talent can connect to career advisors, upskilling, and meaningful job opportunities. Key programs include Equal Access of Chicago, Upbound Staffing/Autism Alliance of Michigan, and DirectConnect Milwaukee.
Ms. Dolan has served as Vice President of Communications and Programs for the Chicagoland Chamber of Commerce, Director at TalentEi, and numerous sales and marketing management roles in the Microsoft partner channel for over twenty years.
David Douglas
Founder & CEO
yolBe Inc.
David Douglas is the Founder & CEO of Yolobe Inc. He has devoted his entire professional career to helping clients maximize the value of their strategic IT initiatives. David is an expert on creating environments that help inspire and encourage both individuals and teams to higher levels of performance. It is this work, coupled with a belief that the Social web, used correctly, can have an incredibly positive impact on individuals and communities, that has led to the founding of Yolobe.
Prior to founding Yolobe, David started two other companies. He helped start the Data Warehousing practice at American Management Systems and was one of the first consultants to join IBM’s Business Intelligence practice. He graduated with a BSFS degree in International Finance from the Edmund A. Walsh School of Foreign Service of Georgetown University and L’Institut d’Etudes Politiques (Sciences Po).

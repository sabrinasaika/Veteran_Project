RSA Reallots FFY 2025 Formula Grant Funds
On August 28, 2025, RSA completed FFY 2025 reallotment for its formula grant programs authorized under the Rehabilitation Act:
- 17 VR agencies relinquished a total of $196,532,641 in VR program funds. After including the maintenance of effort reductions from the FFY 2025 awards ($18,062,284), the total reallotment pool was $214,594,925. Further, 32 VR agencies requested a total of $339,283,350 in reallotment funds. As a result, the amount of funds requested in reallotment exceeded the amount of funds relinquished by $142,750,709. RSA reallotted funds based on the statutory formula to the 32 VR agencies. For specific results, please visit VR Program Reallotment.
- 11 VR agencies relinquished a total of $1,312,263 in Supported Employment-A funds and the same amount in Supported Employment-B funds. When including the FFY 2025 Supported Employment funds that VR agencies did not accept ($969,658), the total Supported Employment reallotment pool was $3,594,184. 15 VR agencies requested a total of $20,094,530 ($10,047,265 for Supported Employment-A and $10,047,265 for Supported Employment-B) in reallotment funds. Therefore, the amount of funds requested exceeded the amount of funds available by $16,500,346. RSA reallotted funds based on the statutory formula to the 15 VR agencies. For specific results, please visit Supported Employment Reallotment.
- Two VR agencies relinquished a total of $243,912 in IL-OIB funds while 21 VR agencies requested a total of $6,730,116 in reallotment funds. The amount of OIB funds requested exceeded the amount available by $6,486,204. Each of the 21 VR agencies received a $5,000 minimum supplement which reduced the pool of available reallotment funds by $105,000. RSA then used the statutory formula to award the remainder of relinquished funds ($138,912). For specific results, please visit IL-OIB Reallotment.
- In FFY 2025, no CAP or PAIR grantee relinquished any formula grant funds. As a result, RSA did not conduct reallotment for these programs.
RSA Celebrates the 35th Anniversary of the ADA
When it became law on July 26, 1990, the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) set forth rights and protections for millions of people with disabilities in the United States. On July 26, 2025, RSA joins ED in celebrating the 35th anniversary of the ADA and the continuation of those rights and protections. Learn more about the ADA from HHS' ADA National Network.

ED and DOL Update WIOA Performance T.A.
On July 10, 2025, ED and DOL updated technical assistance related to the WIOA performance accountability system. With these revisions, the Departments align the technical assistance with changes made by the WIOA Effectiveness in Serving Employers Performance Indicator final rule (89 FR 13814). RSA reissued:
- TAC 19-01: Guidance for Validating Jointly Required Performance Data Submitted under WIOA (originally published December 19, 2018). This joint technical assistance provides information about the guidelines States must use in developing procedures for ensuring the data submitted are valid and reliable, as required by section 116(d)(5) of WIOA.
- TAC 17-04: Guidance on the use of Supplemental Wage Information to implement the Performance Accountability Requirements under WIOA (originally published June 1, 2017). This joint technical assistance recognizes that, in some circumstances, a quarterly wage record from a State Unemployment Insurance (UI) record match may not be possible. In order to ensure that programs may track the participants for performance accountability purposes (if their information is not contained in the State’s UI quarterly wage record system), the Joint WIOA Final Rule permits States to use “other information as is necessary to measure the progress of those participants through methods other than quarterly wage record information” if quarterly wage records are not available for a participant.

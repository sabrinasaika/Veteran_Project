Increasing Technology Awareness and Education During Pre-Employment Transition Services
Presented By:
Teresa Belluscio, Disability Program Administrator, Human Development Institute, University of Kentucky
Carolyn Cage, Training Associate, CTI
Matt Luther, M.Ed., Transition Counselor, Department for Aging and Rehabilitative Services
Vedette Spellman, Department Chair, B.T. Washington HS, Norfolk Public Schools
Date:
February 26, 2025
View Webcast
A five-year study by VCU Transition RRTC, funded by the National Institute on Disability, found that Pre-ETS is essential for students with significant disabilities (ages 14-16). This led to a specialized toolkit to support Pre-ETS. While technology training isn’t a main focus of Pre-ETS, pilot results showed it’s crucial for engaging these students. This presentation, including a roundtable with researchers, a teacher, and a vocational rehabilitation counselor, will discuss technology challenges and barriers to access for students with high support needs.

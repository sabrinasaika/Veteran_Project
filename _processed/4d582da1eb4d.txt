RE:  Revised Federal Fiscal Year 2021 Maintenance of Effort Deficit 
 
Dear Cheryl Fuller: 
On July 19, 2023, the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) issued a letter to the Texas 
Workforce Commission Vocational Rehabilitation Division (TWC-VR) regarding its failure to 
satisfy the maintenance of effort (MOE) requirement under the State Vocational Rehabilitation 
Services (VR) program during Federal fiscal year (FFY) 2021 and, as a result, of the need to 
reduce the TWC-VR award for FFY 2024 by $2,025,191.00, the amount of the MOE shortfall. 
RSA calculated the MOE shortfall based on information contained in the Federal Financial 
Reports (SF-425/RSA-17) submitted by TWC-VR at the time the letter was issued. 
 
After receiving RSA’s letter, TWC-VR notified RSA of potential errors it entered in the 
Rehabilitation Services Administration Management Information System (RSAMIS) to its 4
th 
through 8th (Final) Quarter FFY 2021 RSA-17 financial reports. TWC-VR explained that it had 
incorrectly reported $2,025,191.00 of non-Federal expenditures after the 4th Quarter on line 33 
that should have been reported in the 4th Quarter on line 28. After TWC-VR submitted 
documentation to RSA, which RSA determined sufficient to verify the expenditures were 
obligated during the FFY 2021 year of appropriation, RSA made the relevant reports available to 
TWC-VR to enter corrections, which the agency completed. RSA determined that the State’s and 
agency’s accounting records supported the requested revisions.  
 
Based upon the corrections made, TWC-VR’s FFY 2021 MOE deficit was reduced by 
$2,025,191.00, the full amount of the previously determined shortfall. Therefore, RSA will not 
reduce TWC-VR’s FFY 2024 VR award by $2,025,191.00. See attached table titled “Revised 
MOE Analysis for FFY 2021” for details. 
TWC-VR’s internal control processes failed to identify the reporting error until the agency was 
notified of the MOE deficit by RSA. Internal controls must provide for the “reliability of 
reporting for internal and external use” (34 C.F.R. § 361.12). In the VR services portion of the 
Unified or Combined State Plan, you assured RSA that TWC-VR would employ methods of 
administration to ensure the proper and efficient administration of the VR program. These 
methods of administration (i.e., the agency’s internal controls) must include procedures to ensure

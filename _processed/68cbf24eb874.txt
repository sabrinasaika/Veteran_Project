This series provides success tips and helpful hints to help vocational rehabilitation hearing officers and participants prepare for, conduct, and write final decisions for administrative fair hearings. It can also assist in preparing individuals who wish to represent themselves in a hearing.
One module can help VR staff, who are not attorneys, present their agency’s case at an administrative fair hearing. The final module can help to prepare individuals who have been called as witnesses for what to expect at a hearing
The entire series includes six modules:
The Role of Independent Hearing Officer
Maintaining Hearing Room Decorum
Researching and Writing Decisions
Fair Hearing Guide for VR Staff
Pro Se VR Customer Hearing Tips
Quick Tips for Independent Hearing Witnesses

Sean O'Brien
Achieving a Common Understanding of Customized Employment
Presented By:
Sean O'Brien, M.A., CRC, TA/Training Program Coordinator, The Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Management (VTRAC-QM)
Date:
April 28, 2021
View Webcast
This webcast will address the key elements of Customized Employment (CE) according to the “Essential Elements of CE for Universal Application” which is helping ensure CE is understood and implemented consistently across the country with high quality.  The presenter will also cover other tools and resources that are available to help ensure CE is implemented and sustained as a high quality service delivery option for those who need it.
* This webcast is co-sponsored by The Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Management (VTRAC-QM) amd the VCU DRRP on Customized Employment.

QE Success Stories: Expanding Employment for All
In New Mexico, Stuart, the Director of Operations for Santa Fe Trails Bus System, brought innovation and commitment to TACQE’s Transportation Community of Practice. With monthly meetings and a collaborative Teams channel, the group shared resources and strategies to solve one of the biggest employment barriers: transportation.
Stuart’s contributions improved local accessibility while inspiring peers across states to apply similar solutions. TACQE hopes to highlight these practices in a future webinar.

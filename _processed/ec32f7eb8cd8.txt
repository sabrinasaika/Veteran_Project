Consultative Employment Training and Supports Fidelity Scale (CETSFS)
Presented By:
Steve Hall, Ph.D., Director of Employment Policy and Research, Griffin-Hammis Associates
Date:
August 18, 2021
View Webcast
The Consultative Employment Training and Supports Fidelity Scale (CETSFS) was developed to ensure the entire pyscho-social habilitation and rehabilitation service known as customized employment is completed from beginning to end (Discovery, Customized Job Development, and Consultative Employment Training and Supports) with fidelity to best customized employment practices. When consultative employment training and supports are delivered with fidelity to best practices, the new employee will more likely be employed in their ideal conditions of employment, within a culture of support and reciprocity, that lasts.

RE: Federal Fiscal Year 2020 Maintenance of Effort Calculations 
 
Dear Keith Ozols and Dacia Johnson: 
 
On May 12, 2025, the Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA) issued a letter to the State 
of Oregon (Oregon) regarding it not satisfying the maintenance of effort (MOE) requirement 
under the State Vocational Rehabilitation Services (VR) program during Federal fiscal year 
(FFY) 2020 and, as a result, of the need to reduce the Oregon Commission for the Blind’s (OCB) 
grant for FFY 2026 by $1,428,007, the amount of the MOE shortfall. RSA calculated the MOE 
shortfall based on information contained in the Federal Financial Reports (SF-425/RSA-17) 
submitted by OCB and the Oregon Department of Human Services at the time the letter was 
issued. 
 
After receiving RSA’s letter, OCB notified RSA that the final SF-425 report for FFY 2020 had 
been entered and there should not have been a MOE deficit. However, OCB’s internal controls 
failed to identify that the agency had not actually submitted the report in the RSA Management 
Information System. The report was in partially saved status for several weeks. OCB’s internal 
controls for financial reports should include a verification of submission component to ensure 
there are no similar issues in the future.  
 
Once notified by RSA of the MOE deficit, OCB immediately completed and submitted the FFY 
2020 financial report. Upon receipt of the data, RSA recalculated Oregon’s FFY 2023 MOE and 
determined that the State had met the requirement. Therefore, there will be no reduction to 
OCB’s FFY 2026 VR award to address a MOE shortfall. See attached table titled “Revised MOE 
Analysis for FFY 2020” for details.

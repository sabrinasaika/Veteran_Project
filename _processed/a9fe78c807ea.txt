Introducing the Vocational Evaluation State-to-State Vanguard Group and What It Can Do for You
About this Session
In this training, you'll gain a better understanding of the value and quality that is added to a client being able to make an informed decision on a career path based on the insights they take away from the evaluation process. The power of empowerment, as we like to say.
Learning Objectives:
Understand how vocational evaluation contributes to more successful outcomes.
Name at least three state VR objectives that State-To-State vanguard group addresses.
State the ultimate goal of the vocational evaluation State-To-State vanguard group.

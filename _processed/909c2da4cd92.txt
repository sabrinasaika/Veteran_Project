Progress of ASD Nest Students Over Time - Part 3
Presented By:
Chinah Jewell, Special Education Teacher, Franklin County Public Schools
Kristen Riley, Speech and Language Pathologist, Franklin County Public Schools
Shari Caston, MAT, BCBA, LBA, Autism Specialist, Franklin County Public Schools
Date:
March 24, 2020
View Webcast
It takes a village!  In this village, our Nest students continue to thrive!  In 2016, three, third grade students were the first to participate in the ASD Nest Program in FCPS.   Today, those three students are in the 6th grade at Benjamin Franklin Middle School and continue to be fully included.  In this module, Chinah and Kristen will share with you the outcome of using evidence based practices in an ASD Nest classroom as well as Social Development Intervention (SDI).  Since its inception, we have added an additional ASD Nest classroom and hope to add more in the future!

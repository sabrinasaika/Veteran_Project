Evidence to Employment: Transition Research in Action
This brand new podcast is where research meets real life, and the future of work for youth with disabilities is being reimagined. In every episode, we feature researchers, educators, or youth to unpack the stories, strategies, and data that are driving this work. Join us to learn more about the innovative efforts of the VCU-RRTC on Employment Among Transition-Age Youth with Disabilities.

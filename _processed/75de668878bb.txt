Overview
WIOA Unified and Combined State Plans outline what states and territories are doing to help Americans, including those with barriers to employment, such as individuals with disabilities, into high-quality careers. State Plans also detail how states and territories are helping businesses hire and retain skilled workers. Approved State Plans and modifications are available at the WIOA State Plan Portal.
Joint State Plan Guidance
For each four-year State Plan and the required two-year modification, the Departments issue joint policy guidance outlining the current Administration's priorities for the public workforce system and instructions for submission.
- RSA-TAC-26-01: Modification Requirements for WIOA State Plans for PYs 2026 and 2027 (January 26, 2026)
- RSA-TAC-24-02: Requirements for WIOA State Plans for PYs 2024-2027 (October 31, 2023)
- RSA-TAC-22-02: Modification Requirements for WIOA State Plans for PYs 2022 and 2023 (December 14, 2021)
- RSA-TAC-20-01: Technical Assistance regarding the Submission Deadline for the Unified and Combined State Plans under WIOA for PYs 2020-2023 and Other Related Requirements (February 3, 2020)
- RSA-TAC-18-01: Two-Year Modification Requirements for WIOA Unified and Combined State Plans (January 19, 2018)
- RSA-PD-16-03: WIOA Requirements for Unified and Combined State Plans (March 3, 2016)
State Plan Information Collection Request (ICR)
The Departments collaborate to administer the joint State Plan Requirements ICR (OMB Control # 1205-0522). On December 1, 2025, OMB approved a nonsubstantive change to the ICR and extended approval through March 31, 2027.
State Plan Portal
States are required to submit State Plans and modifications, via the online portal at https://wioaplans.dol.gov/, in accordance with the State Plan Requirements ICR. For assistance with portal access or other questions, please email wioa.plan@dol.gov.
State Plan Technical Assistance
The Departments have developed several technical assistance tools related to State Plans; please visit WorkforceGPS.
VR Services Portion Technical Assistance
RSA's technical assistance center, the VRTAC-QM, hosted a community of practice for the PY 2026 modifications to State Plans with an emphasis on the VR services portion descriptions that require updating.

The Council of State Governments partnered with the Urban Institute to host a virtual conversation sharing the benefits of public sector apprenticeships. Learn about how states can lead the way in implementing this workforce development initiative, and engage with state leaders from Kentucky and California as they share the success of public sector apprenticeships in their states.

The National Technical Assistance Center on Blindness and Low Vision works to improve employment outcomes for individuals with blindness or low vision. They provide free resources and helpful information to businesses, service providers, and people with blindness or low vision.
Read their stories about successful employment of people with blindness or low vision.
Developed by the
National Research and Training Center on Blindness and Low Vision
(NRTC) at Mississippi State University.

Fiscal Calculators
This
Match Calculator
may be used for calculating both
Non-Federal Share Based on Grant Award Amount
and
Maximum Amount of Federal Funds Potentially Available Based on Non-Federal Share Amount
under the VR program.
This
Pre-ETS Reserve Calculator
may be used to calculate the estimated amount of funds required to be reserved by a State for pre-employment transition services, based on Non-Federal and Federal fund variables, and to assess the progress of a single VR agency or a State with two VR agencies toward meeting the reserve requirement.

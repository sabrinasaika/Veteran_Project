About this Event:
The webinar “Working with VR Customers with Criminal Backgrounds” is designed to build and extend on the
Reentry and Rehabilitation
webinar
by providing vocational rehabilitation (VR) practitioners with information on how to most effectively work with and assist individuals that identify as having a disability and a criminal background.
The webinar is intended to inform State VR agency and related personnel about the importance of using a strengths-based approach to career counseling for this specialized population.  The webinar addresses post-incarceration employment policy, as well as preparation of materials and interviewing techniques to discuss past convictions.
Webinar Learning Objectives:
At the conclusion of this webinar, attendees will have increased understanding of:
Unique considerations when working with VR customers with criminal backgrounds
Post-incarceration employment policy and related resources, including the Restoration of Rights Project
Criminal background disclosure

Meet Our Presenters!
Bryan Austin
,
Ph.D., LPC, CRC, CVE
VRTAC-QE Project Consultant
University of Illinois-Urbana Champaign
Dr. Bryan Austin is a consultant for the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center-Quality Employment project at the University of Illinois-Urbana Champaign and has worked in the field of rehabilitation and disability for 25 years. He earned his doctorate in Rehabilitation Counselor Education from Michigan State University, is a Licensed Professional Counselor in the State of Idaho, and Certified Rehabilitation Counselor. Most notably, Dr. Austin has worked as an educator, researcher, vocational rehabilitation counselor, consultant, program coordinator, and project director
Chelsea Greco
,
PhD
Postdoctoral Research Associate
University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC)
As a postdoctoral research associate at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), Chelsea Greco’s research focuses on vocational rehabilitation, health, and career development of individuals with disabilities and chronic health conditions. She recently defended her dissertation at UIUC which examines core areas of career development and employment to improve upon rehabilitation and reentry outcomes for individuals with disabilities who have been incarcerated.

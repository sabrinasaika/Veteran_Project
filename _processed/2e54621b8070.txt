Use of FRRP Funds
Explain briefly how the SLA will distribute the FRRP funds to blind vendors to offset losses incurred during the 2020 calendar year (e.g., proportional to losses incurred, based on losses due to vendor’s spoiled or expired stock, etc.).
FRRP funds will be expended based on a tiered system and will be determined based on the individual net profit loss of each eligible blind vendor within the Business Enterprises Program (BEP).
Step 1, Determine Amount Available:  FRRP award of $906,516 has been appropriated to the SLA to be distributed to California BEP vendors to offset the losses they experienced in 2020.
Step 2, Determine Vendor Eligibility:  The SLA will determine vendor eligibility for FRRP by verifying that a vendor managed a BEP facility in 2020; has an ongoing location in the program; and had a loss of net earnings for 2020 compared to 2019.
Step 3, Determine Vendor Loss in 2020:  The SLA will determine the loss for each eligible BEP vendor that occurred during the calendar year 2020 by calculating the total net income as reported on line 40 of the DR 478 (Vendor’s Monthly Operating Report) for calendar years 2019 and 2020.  The SLA will add any Fair Minimum Return (FMR) funds the vendor received for 2020 to that year’s net income.  After adding the FMR payments, liability insurance claims paid to the vendor, and any other compensation that the vendor reports on their Vendor Attestation, if the vendor’s 2020 net income remains below the net income from 2019, the vendor will be eligible to receive an equitable portion of the FRRP up to the amount of loss of income for 2020.  The intention is to distribute FRRP equitably between all qualified vendors (not to exceed their losses).
Step 4, Calculate the FRRP Payment for Vendors:  The SLA will calculate the FRRP amount for each eligible vendor based on their losses incurred in calendar year 2020.  The fund amount available will be divided by the number of eligible vendors to obtain the maximum available for each vendor.  If some vendors are not eligible for the maximum amount, then those vendors will get the amount of their loss.  The funds remaining from those vendors’ shares will be divided by the new number of eligible vendors who have not been compensated for their total loss of earnings.  These vendors will receive the additional funds up to their maximum.
Step 5, Distribute the FRRP Payment:  The SLA will mail FRRP checks to eligible vendors with a goal of distributing funds within 90 days of receipt of funds from RSA.

How will the SLA ensure blind vendor losses were not previously compensated (e.g., attestation, financial reports, inventory reports, documentation of losses due to spoilage, etc.)?
The Elected Committee of Blind Vendors requested to define “otherwise compensated” as only what is under the purview of the SLA, such as revenues reported on the vendor’s BEP Monthly Operating Reports to the program, the FMR payments, and  liability insurance payments, if any, received by vendors for claims filed for 2020 losses. The SLA supports this request in part; but also understands that BEP vendors may have received compensation for financial losses in 2020 that the SLA is not privy to. With that in mind, the SLA included a field in the Vendor Attestation form that provides vendors an opportunity to report other compensation for consideration in calculating their share of the FRRP allocation.
The SLA has created a Vendor Attestation form for each blind vendor to sign confirming their net profit loss in 2020 and giving them an opportunity to report any other compensation received for their loss. The Vendor Attestation form is attached for RSA’s review.

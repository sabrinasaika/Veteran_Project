The Rural Community Rehabilitation Program + Technology Toolkit provides practical information and guides for employment specialists, vocational rehabilitation counselors, teachers, and other professionals serving supported employees with IDD in rural areas on how to use technology to improve supported employment service delivery.
Justice-Involved Toolkit
The primary purpose of the Justice-Involved Toolkit is to serve as a resource for vocational rehabilitation counselors. The toolkit will also be useful for other practitioners and organizations that work with individuals with disabilities who have been involved with the justice system. It is intended to be both a resource to better understand the needs of justice-impacted adults with disabilities and a tool to improve the provision of services for this disadvantaged population in vocational rehabilitation (VR).
Subminimum Wage Toolkit
The purpose of this toolkit is to provide materials and resources to a variety of invested parties who need to know more about subminimum wage pay.
Business Relations Blueprint
The Business Relations Blueprint Toolkit was developed to enhance the delivery of Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) business services by identifying effective structures, policies, and branding strategies across various State Vocational Rehabilitation Agencies (SVRAs).
VR Outreach Guide
This Outreach Guide presents solutions and strategies to enhance vocational rehabilitation outreach.
Veteran Employment Needs Assessment Tool
This document is crafted to introduce the VRTAC Veteran Employment Needs Assessment Tool, developed by the Veteran Learning Community, designed to assist veterans with disabilities in securing employment.
Customized Training Toolkit
In this toolkit, learn more about the benefits of customized training, customized training strategies, models, steps to developing customized training programs, as well as tips, examples, and applications of customized training.
Self-Employment Toolkit
The Self-Employment in the State-Federal Vocational Rehabilitation System Toolkit is designed to support vocational rehabilitation counselors, consumers, state agencies, and policymakers in navigating self-employment opportunities for individuals with disabilities.

Who We Are
Business Connections, a division of VCU’s School of  
Education, is a Richmond based program dedicated to the hiring,  
advancement, and economic self-sufficiency of people with disabilities. 
For over 30 years, we have specialized in recruitment and retention strategies that are 
customized to meet workforce needs. Our professional staff are highly trained and at 
the top of their field in best practices for supporting individuals with disabilities while 
helping meet business needs.  Let’s partner to build a strong and diverse workforce!
              Our Services are provided at NO 
             cost to employers! 
We Get to Know Your Company      
Business Connections has a broad understanding of many businesses in the Richmond area. 
We make it our mission to spend time in your company and determine your needs in order to 
help you match candidates to the job. 
Qualified Job Candidates      
Business Connections can expand your pool of qualified applicants! We have assessed the 
skills, interests, and abilities of each jobseeker and are prepared to provide any support needs.
Recruitment       
Access to a largely untapped labor source of pre-screened job applicants can reduce the time 
it takes locating dependable workers. We can partner with you to identify creative solutions to 
  address specific staffing needs.
                Job Training       
                       Our professional staff can help identify accommodations and provide individual
                                job skills training as needed to ensure the employee is meeting company 
                                    expectations and production standards. Employers report 
                                                enhanced productivity, dependability, and overall 
                                                                        improved moral.

Retention       
We realize it is not enough to simply find qualified candidates. Keeping talented 
employees on the job and reducing the costly problems associated with turnover 
is a primary goal of our program.
Business Training and Support      
Business Connections can provide training and support to you and your staff on 
topics related to Disability Awareness, ADA, and Business Tax Credits.

This webcast is titled Learn to Earn: The Effect of Paid Employment During High School on Competitive Integrated Employment and Community Engagement in Adulthood.  In this webcast, Lead Investigator Whitney Ham, Ph.D., Researcher Hannah Carignan, M.A., CRC, and Research Steering Committee Member Ann Marie Chevalier share an overview of the research and its potential impact on the field of transition research. Join us to learn about this study examining the effect of high school employment on Competitive Integrated Employment (CIE), community engagement, and quality of life after high school.

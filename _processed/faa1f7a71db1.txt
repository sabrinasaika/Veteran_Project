About this Event:
Join our webinar, where we will delve into the underlying reasons for unemployment among veterans with disabilities.
You will have the opportunity to explore the Veteran Learning Community Employment Tool and gain hands-on experience using this innovative tool to support veterans in their employment journey.
▸

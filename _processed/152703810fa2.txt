RRTC Podcasts
VCU-RRTC hosts two podcasts, one for the RRTC on the Employment of Persons with Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities, and one for the RRTC on Employment Among Transition-Age Youth with Disabilities. Both podcasts have their own webpages linked below, and both can be found on various platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube.
RRTC-IDD: Zooming into Focus
This podcast features interviews with the project's director, research study leads, and steering committee members discussing the research and training of the project, and also with individuals with Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities (IDD) discussing their lived experiences with employment. The new podcast will release episodes on the second Monday of each month over the next five years.
RRTC-Transition: Evidence to Employment: Transition Research in Action
Welcome to Evidence to Employment: Transition Research in Action, the podcast where research meets real life, and the future of work for youth with disabilities is being reimagined. In every episode, we feature researchers, educators, or youth to unpack the stories, strategies, and data that are driving this work. Join us to learn more about the innovative efforts of the VCU-RRTC on Employment Among Transition-Age Youth with Disabilities.

Iowa Labor Market Information Training - Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment
▸
About this training
Learn more about why labor market information is important to use in VR, how to find it, and how to access specific labor market information for your state. We'll look at economic trends and the labor market, the impact of the pandemic, and the economic recovery. Then we'll wrap up by talking about labor trends and employment projections.

For aspiring, new, or seasoned supervisors, this training will provide practical skills, knowledgeable insights, and proven practices to improve your vocational rehabilitation counselor supervision skills.
Over the course of seven training modules, James Herbert, a 35 year Rehabilitation Counselor Educator and 30 year Clinical supervision researcher, will provide a foundation for increasing your knowledge and skills, enhancing counselor performance, and improving client outcomes.
The contents of this presentation were developed with support from the
Center for Innovative Training in Vocational Rehabilitation (CIT-VR).

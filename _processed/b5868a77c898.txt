Name
Kelley
Body
Kelley’s journey to a fulfilling career was made possible by
New Hampshire’s VR (VRNH) agency
and
Services for the Blind and Vision Impaired (SBVI)
. VR supported Kelley in earning a Bachelor’s degree in Communication with a focus on Professional Writing from Southern New Hampshire University. The agency provided essential services, including an 8-week CVS Health Work from Home Internship where she worked as an Administrative Assistant. This reintroduced her to the corporate environment after a 20-year gap and VR facilitated necessary accommodations and equipment. Kelley is now successfully employed full-time in a remote role with CVS’s Aetna division. Click
here
to learn more about Kelley’s journey.

April 26, 2021
We, the elected Committee of Licensed Managers of the Indiana BEP, appointed a sub-committee to work with the Indiana Family and Social Services Administration Blind and Visually Impaired Services (the SLA)to develop a plan for the distribution and spending of the FRRP funds. The subcommittee met with the SLA staff to decide on the best way to calculate a manger’s loss, how to spend extra funds and what information to put into our documents before sending them to RSA.  As committee chairperson I was in ongoing communication with the SLA about what other states were doing in regard to their plans. The SLA then presented us with a draft proposal for our approval.  We requested some minor changes that were agreed to leading us to approve the final documents. We appreciate the Sla and the way they worked with us throughout this process.

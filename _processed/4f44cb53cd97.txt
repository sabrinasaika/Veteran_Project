This session will introduce participants to the concept of work-based learning and the benefits as they apply to students with disabilities. Special focus will be placed on opportunities to build independence and employment skills across environments. Presenters will provide tools and ideas for building opportunities in the home, school, and community, including techniques and strategies for approaching and collaborating with partners.
This training is provided by the Center on Transition Innovations at Virginia Commonwealth University.

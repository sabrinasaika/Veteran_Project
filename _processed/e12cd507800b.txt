This is Part 2 of Recruitment Strategies for Hiring Individuals with Disabilities.  Before the recruitment process starts, there should be some building blocks in place.  For successful strategies for hiring individuals with disabilities, there should be buy-in from the top down.  In this presentation, commitment, accommodation policies, application and screening processes, training, and creative interview practices are some of the topics discussed.
This training is provided by the
Virginia Commonwealth University Rehabilitation Research and Training Center (VCU-RRTC)

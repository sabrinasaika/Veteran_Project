Looking for ways to grow professionally and increase quality employment outcomes for people with disabilities?
VRTAC-QE, the Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment, now offers training in effective vocational rehabilitation (VR) practices and service delivery. Training is free, but requires you to create an account to participate. Certified Rehabilitation Counselor (CRC) credits are available for select trainings.
Current VRTAC-QE Training Opportunities
Current VRTAC-QE training opportunities include:
Customized Employment
Vocational Rehabilitation Services for Hispanic Individuals with Disabilities
Customized Training
Outreach to Rural and Migrant Communities
Motivational Interviewing Introduction
More Training Available and Coming Soon!
Check the
VRTAC-QE training page
often for new VRTAC-QE training opportunities, information on other training events; and access to VRTAC-QE’s library of on-demand training.
About VRTAC-QE
VRTAC-QE’s goal is to increase the knowledge and skills of state VR agencies and community partners that help people with disabilities achieve quality employment and career advancement, with emphasis on competitive integrated employment.
Visit VRTAC-QE to learn more.

TITLE: RSA Annual Reports to the President and Congress | Rehabilitation Services Administration
URL: https://rsa.ed.gov/about/rsa-annual-reports-to-congress

The Annual Reports to the President and Congress describe the activities of RSA as the principal agency carrying out Titles I, III, VI, and specified portions of Titles V and VII of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended by the Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act. RSA is responsible for preparing and submitting these reports under Section 13 of the Rehabilitation Act.

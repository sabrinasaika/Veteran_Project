WIOA Policy Guidance
For the VR program, RSA issues joint policy guidance with its Federal partners at the U.S. Department of Labor's
Employment and Training Administration
(ETA) and the U.S. Department of Education's
Office of Career, Technical, and Adult Education
(OCTAE).
As a result, these policies also apply to the other five WIOA core programs:
Adult, Dislocated Worker, and Youth programs authorized under WIOA title I and administered by ETA;
Adult Education and Family Literacy Act program authorized under WIOA title II and administered by OCTAE; and
Employment Service program authorized under the Wagner-Peyser Act, as amended by WIOA title III, and administered by ETA.
Please visit
Sub-Regulatory Guidance
for the latest joint policy guidance implementing WIOA and its joint regulations.

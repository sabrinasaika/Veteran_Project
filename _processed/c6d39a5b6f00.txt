As we step into 2025, take a look at the tools and guides that vocational rehabilitation professionals like you relied on most in 2024. These resources are designed to help you break down barriers, innovate strategies, and empower people with disabilities to achieve meaningful, quality employment.
The QE Toolkits Collection
Our newest and most popular toolkits use feedback from VR rehabilitation counselors, VA rehabilitation counselors, and community agency rehabilitation professionals. These resources are built on implementation science to address the challenges faced by people with disabilities seeking employment.
Explore these toolkits to tackle disability employment challenges
.
VR Outreach Guide
Barriers can make access difficult, but this guide provides practical ways to connect with people with disabilities and meet their needs of specific populations.
Discover strategies for effective VR outreach
.
Supported and Customized Employment Side by Side Referral Decision Guide
Not sure whether supported or customized employment is the best fit? This guide explains both approaches, who they’re for, and includes easy-to-follow charts to guide your decisions.
Check out this decision-making tool
.
Comprehensive Review – VRTAC-QE White Paper
This research-based review identifies proven strategies to improve career readiness and employment outcomes. It’s a must-read for staying updated on best practices.
Learn about key findings and recommendations
.
Research Summary: Re-framing Workplace Inclusion Through the Lens of Universal Design
Universal design makes workplaces more inclusive for everyone. This resource explores how to apply these principles in schools, work, and communities.
Read more about universal design and inclusion
.
Resource Guide: Disaster Preparedness
Help people with disabilities stay safe and continue working toward their goals during emergencies. This guide offers expert tips and strategies for disaster planning.
Get guidance on disaster preparedness
.
Resource Guide: Employer Engagement
Many employers want to hire people with disabilities but need support to understand inclusion. This guide helps you connect employers with qualified candidates and improve employment outcomes.
Learn more about connecting employers with qualified candidates
.
Webinar: Employees with Disabilities Profiler
Discover how this unique tool can help businesses enhance their hiring and retention strategies for employees with disabilities.
Join this webinar to promote employment of people with disabilities
.
Resource Guide: Work Incentive Benefits Counseling
Many people with disabilities hesitate to work, fearing they’ll lose benefits. This guide explains how to connect the people you serve to critical resources that empower them to make informed choices.
Learn how to connect clients with work incentives benefits counseling
.
Enhancing Remote VR Services with Smart Tech and AI
Discover how remote work, AI, and new tech can improve VR services and job outcomes. Yolbe, a TACQE partner, shares practical tips to help VR professionals adapt to a changing workforce.
Learn how tech is transforming vocational services
.

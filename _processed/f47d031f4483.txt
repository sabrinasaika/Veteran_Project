Training Group Tips
How to Enroll in Your Training Group
Each training group will receive a unique link to log into your group training. If you do not have a link, please ask your supervisor or contact us. It will look something like this: http://tacqe.com/login/?tgid=TRAINING_GROUP_ID.
When you click on the link, you will be asked to log in or create an account for the TACQE.com website.
Once you have created your account and logged in, you will be brought to a Training Group Enrollment page, where you will click the "Enroll Now" button to access the training group.
Once you click the "Enroll Now" button you will be directed to your training group page.

How to Return to Your Training
If you are not logged in:
Log in
.
You will be taken to your Dashboard
Scroll down to the "My Training" section
Your trainings will be listed there. If you have already completed the evaluation(s) for a training, it will be moved to the "Completed" tab.
If you are already logged in and cannot find the dashboard:
From the navigation menu at the top of any page, click on Account > My Dashboard.
Scroll down to the "My Training" section
Your trainings will be listed there. If you have already completed the evaluation(s) for a training, it will be moved to the "Completed" tab.
TIP:
Go directly to the training section on the dashboard by clicking here:
http://tacqe.com/dashboard/#training
, and then bookmark it for later.

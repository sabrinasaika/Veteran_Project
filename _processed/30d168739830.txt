About this Event:
For job seekers, re-entering the workforce can be an adjustment. Having the resources and supports available both pre- and post- job placement can make that transition easier. Join us as we provide an overview of the Ticket to Work Partnership Plus program. We’ll discuss the potential roles vocational rehabilitation agencies and Employment Networks play in the job placement process and explore how organizations can work together toward successful client outcomes. We’ll also share information about statewide best practices, including resources for strengthening your relationship with your statewide Partnership Plus coordinator and local Employment Networks.
Learning Objectives:
Provide an overview of Partnership Plus – what it is and how it works!
Share information about statewide best practices leading to successful job placement outcomes.
Discuss strategies and resources for connecting and strengthening the Partnership Plus program locally.

QE Success Stories: Expanding Employment for All
When VR Counselor Dee Franzen first met Tyler Fech, she saw his determination to own a business. TACQE training gave Dee the strategies to support customized self-employment, and Tyler pursued his dream of a secure shredding company.
Today, Tyler proudly runs
TJ Shredzz
, surrounded by a supportive network of family, friends, and partners.
“I am a graduate, an athlete, a business owner, and a person with a disability. I don’t let my disability define me… I strive to exceed expectations every day.” — Tyler Fech

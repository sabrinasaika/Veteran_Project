Rehabilitation Services Administration (RSA)
Timeline of WIOA Annual Reports for the Vocational Rehabilitation (VR) Program
Program Year 2017
State VR programs reported WIOA data for the first time following the completion of Program Year 2017. These reports only include performance results for Measurable Skill Gains (MSG) Rate because this was the only indicator for which data were available for reporting and represent gains that participants achieved during Program Year 2017.
Program Year 2018
State VR programs reported performance across three indicators:
MSG Rate (participants during PY 2018);
Employment Rate in the Second Quarter after Exit (participants who exited during PY 2017); and
Median Earnings in the Second Quarter after Exit (participants who exited during PY 2017)
Program Year 2019
State VR programs reported performance across five indicators for the first year:
MSG Rate (participants during PY 2019);
Employment Rate in the Second Quarter after Exit (participants who exited during PY 2018);
Median Earnings in the Second Quarter after Exit (participants who exited during PY 2018);
Employment Rate in the Fourth Quarter after Exit (participants who exited during CY 2018); and
Credential Attainment Rate (participants who exited during CY 2018)

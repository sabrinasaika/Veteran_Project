Over the past five years, TACQE has
worked alongside vocational rehabilitation professionals and agencies to strengthen services by sharing practical tools, proven strategies, and high-quality training
. As we count down to the project’s end on September 30, we’re taking a moment to spotlight some of the resources that continue to make a real impact on improving employment outcomes for people with disabilities.
This week, we're highlighting key insights from our most popular training sessions
—covering what resonated most with participants and how these ideas shape everyday practice. Whether you're looking for a fresh approach or a reminder of proven techniques, there’s something here to support your work.
All trainings are free and available online.

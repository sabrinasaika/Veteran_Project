Stefanie Paul, 
                M.Ed., BCBA, LBA
Trainer & Technical Assistance Associate
smpaul@vcu.edu
Stefanie Paul, M.Ed., BCBA, LBA, has been a training and technical assistance associate at the Autism Center for Excellence (ACE) since 2014. Mrs.Paul earned her Masters of Education in Special Education from Old Dominion University. In 2019, she became a Board Certified, Licensed Behavior Analyst in Virginia. She comes to ACE with experience as a lead special education teacher and an instructional coach with over twenty years of experience teaching young students with Autism Spectrum Disorder, in Virginia. Mrs. Paul assists in overseeing, planning, and facilitating Project Preparing for Academic and Social Success within select school divisions in Virginia. Project PASS is a professional development model which aims to improve the implementation of evidence-based practices for young children with ASD in early childhood settings.

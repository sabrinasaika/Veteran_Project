About this Event:
This training module equips participants with the knowledge and skills necessary to implement transitional employment within their agency. It covers the selection criteria for identifying individuals who would benefit most from this level of employment support and explores best practices for providing comprehensive assistance to individuals facing significant barriers to community employment. Participants will gain a nuanced understanding of transitional employment implementation and support strategies tailored to diverse needs, enhancing their ability to facilitate successful employment transitions for those they serve.

Clubhouse Generalist

James Van Abel is a Clubhouse Generalist at Yahara House, a Madison Clubhouse-model program for adults with mental illness. Job Squad Member

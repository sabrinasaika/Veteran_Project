The
VRTAC Veteran Employment Needs Assessment Tool
was developed by the
Veteran Learning Community
, designed to assist veterans with disabilities in securing employment.
By leveraging feedback from a wide array of stakeholders, including State-Federal vocational rehabilitation counselors, VA vocational rehabilitation counselors, and community rehabilitation agencies, the tool reflects a robust understanding of the multifaceted challenges veterans with disabilities encounter in their pursuit of employment.
As agencies integrate this tool into their practice, they can better identify and address the unique barriers veterans face, ultimately fostering enhanced vocational outcomes and promoting the holistic well-being of this deserving population.

More about this tool:
Who is this for?
This tool could be utilized by State-Federal vocational rehabilitation counselors, VA vocational rehabilitation counselors, community rehabilitation agencies, leadership from State-Federal VR agencies, leadership from VA VR agencies, and other stakeholders.
How was the toolkit created?
The Toolkit was created under the auspices of the Vocational Rehabilitation Technical Assistance Center for Quality Employment (VRTAC-QE). It was developed by a Learning Community focused on Veterans, comprising vocational rehabilitation professionals from the community, state-federal VR agencies, and VA agencies.

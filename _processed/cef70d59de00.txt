RE: FFY 2021 Annual Review Finding Regarding Noncompliance with Pre-Employment 
Transition Services Requirements 
 
Dear Brent McNeal and Robert Doyle: 
 
This letter presents the Rehabilitation Services Administration’s (RSA) annual review and 
finding regarding the State’s failure to satisfy requirements governing the provision of pre-
employment transition services (pre-ETS) in Federal fiscal year (FFY) 2021, as determined 
by RSA in accordance with Section 107(a) of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (Rehabilitation 
Act).
 Pursuant to Section 107(a)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act, RSA conducts an annual review 
of the Florida Division of Vocational Rehabilitation (DVR) and the Florida Division of Blind 
Services (DBS) to determine whether the State is substantially complying with the 
requirements of its Unified or Combined State Plan, including requirements governing the 
provision of pre-ETS to students with disabilities. The FFY 2021 final financial reports 
submitted by DVR and DBS, the most recent final financial reports available to RSA, 
demonstrate the State failed to satisfy the requirements of Sections 110(d)(1) and 113 of the 
Rehabilitation Act by not expending at least 15 percent of its State Vocational Rehabilitation 
Services (VR) grant amount on the provision of pre-ETS for students with disabilities.  
 
Annual Review of DVR’s and DBS’ FFY 2021 Fiscal Data 
 
Short Summary:   
 
RSA has determined, after conducting an annual review of DVR’s and DBS’ final FFY 2021 
RSA-17 financial reports, pursuant to Section 107(a)(2) of the Rehabilitation Act, that the 
agencies failed to satisfy the requirements of Sections 110(d)(1) and 113(a) of the

Rehabilitation Act (i.e., the expenditure requirements) by not expending at least 15 percent of 
the State’s matched FFY 2021 VR grant funds on the provision of pre-employment transition 
services (pre-ETS) for students with disabilities.  
 
Annual Review Requirements:  
  
Section 107(a)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act requires RSA to conduct annual reviews and 
periodic onsite monitoring of the VR agencies to determine whether they are in substantial 
compliance with their approved State plan, including the VR services portion of that Plan. 
In conducting the annual reviews, RSA must consider, at a minimum, budget, and financial 
management data (Section 107(a)(2)(I) of the Rehabilitation Act). Pursuant to Section 
107(a)(4)(B) of the Rehabilitation Act, RSA must examine the provision of services, 
including pre-ETS.  
 
Analysis of FFY 2021 Financial Data:   
 
According to DVR’s and DBS’ final FFY 2021 RSA-17s, the combined net Federal VR 
allotment for the State was $209,470,376, all of which DVR and DBS matched. Therefore, 
DVR and DBS were required to expend a combined total of at least $31,256,284.17 for the 
provision of pre-ETS. DVR and DBS reported on their respective final FFY 2021 RSA-17s 
spending a combined total of $31,108,941 (14.9 percent of the State’s VR allotment) in 
Federal VR funds for pre-ETS. In addition, DVR had $4,604,561.94 in matched unused 
Federal funds remaining at the end of FFY 2021 and DSB had $3,320,974.47. 
 
RSA’s Annual Review Finding:   
 
As demonstrated by their final RSA-17s for FFY 2021, DVR and DBS expended a combined 
total of 14.9 percent of their matched Federal VR funds, an increase from the 12.3 percent of 
the agencies’ expenditures for pre-ETS in FFY 2020. RSA acknowledges the State continued 
to reserve the additional 0.1 percent of its matched Federal VR funds by leaving the funds 
unspent at the end of the period of performance. 
 
Noncompliance with Approved State Plan:   
 
Section 101(a)(25) of the Rehabilitation Act requires DVR and DBS to assure in their 
Unified or Combined State Plan, with respect to students with disabilities, that the State has 
developed and implemented strategies to: 
 
• address the needs identified in the assessments described in Section 101(a)(15) 
of the Rehabilitation Act; 
• achieve the goals and priorities identified by the State in accordance with Section 
101(a)(15) of the Rehabilitation Act to improve and expand VR services for 
students with disabilities on a statewide basis; and 
• provide pre-ETS.

Pre-ETS, as defined at Section 7(30) of the Rehabilitation Act, “means services provided in 
accordance with section 113.” Section 113(a) of the Rehabilitation Act requires the State to 
ensure that DVR and DBS will use the funds reserved under Section 110(d)(1) of the 
Rehabilitation Act to provide, or arrange for the provision of, pre-ETS to students with 
disabilities. Section 110(d)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act establishes the 15 percent minimum 
reservation requirement for the State from its VR allotment.

Response:  
RSA has found, after conducting an annual review of DVR’s and DBS’ final FFY 2021 
RSA-17s pursuant to Section 107 of the Rehabilitation Act, that the State did not satisfy the 
pre-ETS expenditure requirements that year. Specifically, the State reserved 15 percent of 
its Federal VR funds and spent 14.9 percent on the provision of pre-ETS for students with 
disabilities. 
For purposes of this finding, RSA requires DVR and DBS to continue implementing 
strategies that ensure the State reserves and will expend at least 15 percent of its VR grant 
amount on the provision of pre-employment transition services by September 30, 2024. 
Because the State has reached full compliance with the requirement of Section 110(d)(1) of 
the Rehabilitation Act and, RSA believes, through established strategies, DVR and DBS 
will achieve full compliance with the requirement of Section 113(a) of the Rehabilitation 
Act within a reasonable period of time, RSA requires no further corrective actions or 
submissions. In making this determination, RSA considered that DVR and DBS have made 
significant progress in implementing procedures to bring the State into full compliance 
with the requirement of Section 110(d)(1) of the Rehabilitation Act and almost into full 
compliance with the requirement of Section 113(a) of the Rehabilitation Act. 
 
Please be aware that in subsequent audits or program monitoring RSA will review your 
agencies to ensure continued compliance with the applicable requirements.

Sebastian Mumford
Information Technology Specialist
mumfordsx@vcu.edu
Sebastian is a recent graduate from VCU; he graduated in December 2022 with a bachelor's degree in supply chain management and a minor in psychology. He has been a part of Alpha Phi Alpha Fraternity Incorporated since the fall of 2019 and has held several positions in the organization such as sergeant in arms, treasurer, and secretary. He has been working as an IT support specialist for the VCU IT Support Center since the beginning of 2022 and is now onboarding with the Rehabilitation Research and Training Center in the hopes to expand his knowledge in the IT field to better assist those in need of his service and advance his future. When Sebastian is not working he enjoys turning on a nice Netflix movie/series, playing the piano, or making music in his free time.
